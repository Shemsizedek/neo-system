## Do / Don't
**Do:**
- Lead with lived threads (faith rhythm, acting grind, deal work) before philosophy.
- Keep claims verifiable; mark what's aspirational as aspirational.
- Let the institutional NEO System speak through its own channels; personal channels stay personal.
- Approve every public-facing asset with the founder first.

**Don't:**
- Fabricate achievements, titles, dates, or statistics.
- Mix institutional authority (NEO-GAS, tribunal, canon) into personal brand claims.
- Post, publish, or commit anything in his name without explicit approval.
- Render formal royal/authorial titles in casual public content.
