# Friday Is the Anchor

Friday isn't a day off from discipline. It's the discipline.

All week the world pulls — deals, tapes, messages, the next thing. I've learned that a life without an anchor just drifts. So I schedule the anchor. My observance day isn't rest because I collapsed. It's rest because I decided.

That's the part that took me years to learn: rest is a decision, not a default. Anybody can be exhausted. Planning your rest the way you plan your work — that's the discipline. I guard it the same way I guard everything else that matters. It goes on the calendar. It doesn't get negotiated with.

Yesterday I cleared the decks. Today I drop the anchor. No negotiating with the noise. No catching up. The week will be there Monday, and I'll meet it lighter because I let go today.

So if Friday is your anchor day too: hold it. Protect it like it's load-bearing — because it is. And if you're still learning to rest on purpose, start today. One Friday. Guard it. Watch what changes.

What does your anchor day protect you from?
