# Teach It the Same Day

The fastest way to learn something is to explain it the same day you learned it.

I've got a rule now: whatever I study in the morning [to confirm: current Coursera courses and legal-study areas] has to come back out of my mouth before the day ends. A lesson, a clause, a deal term — doesn't matter. I explain it to my notebook, to a friend, to an empty room if I have to. If I can't explain it, I didn't learn it. I just collected it.

Most of us are collectors. Tabs open. Courses bookmarked. Highlights everywhere. Collecting feels like learning, but it's just inventory. Teaching is the test — it forces the idea out of the fog and into words, and words are where you find out what you actually understood.

So here's the habit: learn one thing today, teach it today. Explain the term like the other person has never heard it. Watch where you stumble — that's the part you didn't actually get. Go back, get it, explain it again.

Fridays are good for this. The pace slows. The morning gives you a lesson; the afternoon gives you the time to teach it back. Learning compounds through teaching, not through collecting tabs.

What's one thing you learned this week that you could teach someone today?
