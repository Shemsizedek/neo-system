# Monday Decides the Week

Most people start Monday reacting. The inbox, the noise, everyone else's urgency. By Wednesday they're spent — and the week happened to them instead of through them.

I've learned the week is won before it starts. Not with a 20-item to-do list, but with one honest question: what does this week need from me? Write it down. One page. The three things that actually move the needle, and everything else waits.

Discipline isn't doing more. It's deciding what matters before the world decides for you. That's the whole game — faith, business, craft. Same muscle, different arena.

My Monday ritual is simple: sit down early, get quiet, write the page, then move. No ceremony. Just a decision that the week has a shape before it has a speed.

So here's my question for you this Monday: if you could only get three things done this week, what would they be? Drop them below. Writing them where others can see them is the first act of discipline.
