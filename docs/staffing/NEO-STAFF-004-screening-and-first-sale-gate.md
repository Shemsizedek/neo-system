**Current status:** ZERO VERIFIED OPT-IN NEO APPLICANTS. No interviews booked, no screening completed, no recruitment emails sent. This checklist is for applicant intake after documented consent.

## Intake process

1. Only accept people who reply to the published GitHub EOI https://github.com/Shemsizedek/neo-system/issues/1373 via verified inbox subject `NEO-STAFF-003 — Talent Interest` or an equivalent verified, consent-based official intake.
2. Log request date, source URL, consent and withdrawal route in the private ATS. Do not place candidate names, emails, resumes or sensitive documents in the public repository.
3. Send an acknowledgment that says "expression of interest, not paid employment offer"; describe eligibility and legal limits. No promised earnings/equity or invented role.
4. Ask for work region/time zone, languages, specific B2B sales case example, portfolio (non-sensitive), permitted sales channels, desired pay arrangement and preferred first project.
5. Use a consistent 5-point rubric, with role-fit/practical ethics not personal/protected characteristics. The preliminary rubric is **screening**, not a licensing or hiring determination:
   - Relevant commercial business development experience (0–5).
   - Credible, verifiable portfolio and professional references (0–5).
   - Ability to sell truthfully without regulated claims (0–5).
   - Privacy, anti-spam and customer-consent judgment (0–5).
   - Fit with *actual* product economics and delivery capability (0–5).
   - **Do not score** protected traits, nationality as a proxy, faith affiliation, disability or age; assess jurisdictional work eligibility separately and lawfully.
6. First potential interview (15–20 min): company and candidate introductions; candidate's prior sale process; qualified leads versus outbound, base pay preferences; ethics scenario; country and tax/worker classification; no offer yet.
7. Any work sample must be a simulated conversation on fictional data, brief and clearly nonproductive, or a **paid** authorized trial if applicable laws and actual scope require it. Do not require real lead harvesting or customer outreach as an unpaid screening step.
8. Only after verification, issue authorized conditional offer and legal contract. Do not grant NEO domain/wallet/API credentials during screening.
