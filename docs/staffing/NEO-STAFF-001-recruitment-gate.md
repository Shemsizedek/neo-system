## Objective

Build an opt-in, global recruiting pipeline that prioritizes **legitimate commercial sales** and **donation program support** while minimizing recurring payroll obligations, preserving labor-law compliance, and documenting all outreach and payments. NEO's project plans are not proof that an employer has opened a funded position, issued security, or received donations.

## Workforce acquisition

1. Publish a truthful, compensation-disclosed opportunity through free or approved employer channels. Prioritize direct opt-in applicants; do not scrape private resumes, evade site rules, or harvest personal emails.
2. Screen fit on geography, language, evidence of prior client acquisition, ethics, product/domain knowledge, availability, and right to work/contract where required.
3. Confirm that candidate contact consent or other lawful basis exists; avoid bulk unsolicited email. Comply with applicable anti-spam and data-protection requirements, including opt-outs and retention.
4. Interview finalists, verify relevant qualifications and sanctions/licensing only when legitimately needed; do not request sensitive identity/banking material before a genuine conditional offer and secure intake.
5. Classify employee vs independent contractor on actual working practices, not the word 'commission', title or contract label. Employees must receive legally required wages, taxes and protections; global jurisdictions reviewed individually.
6. For contractors, agree in writing on defined eligible sale, commission rate, gross-vs-net basis, recognition upon cleared payment, cancellation/refund clawback, payment schedule, currency, taxes, customer attribution, dispute resolution, termination, confidentiality, brand use and restrictions.
7. For token/share/equity compensation, require separate securities, tax, corporate and local employment review; do not promise value, liquidity or ownership before valid authorization and issuance.
8. Create limited-access assignments only **after** signed authorization, security onboarding, approved scripts and destination-specific permissions. Never grant treasury, wallet, source-of-truth, production, privileged data or signing authority by default.
9. Review outcome, reconcile source-to-CRM-to-payment evidence, pay only under approved terms and log performance.

States: `discovered -> permission_verified -> contacted -> applicant -> screened -> finalist -> conditional_offer -> signed -> onboarded -> active -> closed`. Do not use `hired` until an actual signed arrangement is in effect.

## Intake and message rules

- No application fees, mandatory purchases, crypto deposits or promises of minimum earnings.
- Disclose commission-only/variable compensation candidly, and no salary if genuinely none is offered for a lawful contractor arrangement.
- Use a specific product/service, contractual entity, accurate proof of capabilities, and verified payment/fulfillment terms.
- Charity/donation workers must not be paid as a percentage of funds raised (AFP standards). Do not claim donor tax deductibility without verified tax status.
- Do not pitch investments, tokenized equity, profit-sharing arrangements or securities.
- Use human approval for final hiring, compensation plans, binding contracts, institutional fundraising claims or any production publishing under existing NEO policy.
- For commercial email, satisfy the recipient jurisdiction's requirements and the FTC CAN-SPAM rules where applicable. Respect suppression and unsubscribe requests.
