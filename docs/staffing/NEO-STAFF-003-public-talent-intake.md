## Product, sales and donation separation

- `integrations/minicart/neoteric-catalog.yaml` documents founder-approved **$144** prices on some Noological and education offerings. It does not independently prove sale availability, customer checkout, practitioner capacity, invoices, refund obligations, or receipt settlement. The public storefront/checkout could not be independently verified from the current web evidence. This is a **commercial readiness blocker**.
- `apps/neo-counter/README.md` explicitly describes a v0.1 shell and mock/test payment rails, not validated live commission payouts.
- No 15% commission or other percentage is authorized or offered. The Gate 002 15% figure was a scenario assumption only.
- Donations and volunteer community work are distinct from commercial sales. AFP ethics rejects commissions or finder fees calculated as a percentage of charitable gifts. No donation-tax-deductibility promise absent verification.
- Equity, Counterparty/NOMNI tokens, securitized interests, lending and investment solicitation are excluded from launch marketing and staffing engagements. Do not market shares or tokens to applicants as guaranteed compensation or liquid ownership.
- Validate actual employer, country eligibility, labor law, classification, product terms and accounting before any employment, paid work or engagement commitment.
