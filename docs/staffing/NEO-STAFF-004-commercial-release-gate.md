## First commercial sale: minimum acceptance test

1. **Actual seller**: validate legal name, permitted activities, signatory authority, correct PayPal Business recipient, bank/merchant identity and official business communications; a historical EIN letter, trust declaration or internal title alone does not prove a current legal employer.
2. **Offering**: select only non-regulated educational or properly scoped, nonclinical service; validate price and specific deliverables, provider identity, time slots, capacity, cancellation/refund policy and truthful scope. For Noological Dialogue, non-diagnostic means **no clinical claims**.
3. **Customer protection**: customer consent, terms and refund process; separate service-practice information from payment records.
4. **Checkout**: PayPal supports no-code Business Payment Links without a full website; the connected user/business must authenticate and create the correct seller-specific link. https://developer.paypal.com/payment-links-buttons/create-payment-link/ . Fees apply and vary; do not invent a live URL, account ownership or transaction receipt.
5. **Sale evidence**: authorized real purchaser or lawful test mode; provider order ID, SKU, gross receipt, taxes, fees, refunds, net eligible proceeds and proof of delivery. Avoid fake orders or self-pay testing that violates merchant rules.
6. **Worker compensation**: choose and legally approve employee vs independent contractor (IRS behavioral, financial and relationship factors; do not classify by label). Approve the commission agreement before work, or pay required wages as applicable. No earned commissions exist before signed terms and an eligible cleared transaction.
