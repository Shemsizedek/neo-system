## Candidate/offer readiness

1. Responsible contracting entity and employer identity must be confirmed in writing.
2. Eligible product must be fully scoped and commercially fulfillable, with legal market access, support, refund policy, live receipt evidence and approved truthful claims.
3. Commission-only work classification requires country-by-country legal analysis. Texas non-exempt employee wages and overtime are protected even with commission pay; commission-only independent-contractor classification cannot be assumed simply by naming the role 'partner'.
4. A **15% commission** is only an illustrative planning assumption; it is NOT approved, payable or advertised. Before public advertising, approve eligible revenue definition, attribution, refunds/chargebacks, currency, payment dates and calculation method in a signed contract.
5. Fundraisers/donation stewards are a separate volunteer or legally funded wage/fixed-fee lane. Do not pay a percentage of charitable gifts. Verify beneficiary and donation rails, receipt wording and any claims of tax treatment.
6. Tokens, investment securities, equity interests and regulated products are explicitly excluded from initial commercial sales duties pending dedicated securities and labor-law analysis.
7. Candidate solicitation must be opt-in and within platform/anti-spam requirements. Do not scrape private profiles or personal email addresses. Do not send mass mail or charge an application fee.
8. After applicant permission, screen on lawful role criteria and use least-privilege access only after written agreement.
