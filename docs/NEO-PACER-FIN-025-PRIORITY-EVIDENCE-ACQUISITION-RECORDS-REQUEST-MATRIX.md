# NEO-PACER FIN-025 — Priority Evidence Acquisition & Records Request Matrix

## Purpose
FIN-025 converts the claim-by-claim adjudication register into an acquisition plan for primary evidence. Its function is to identify the shortest lawful evidentiary path capable of authenticating, corroborating, contradicting, narrowing, or closing each material claim.

## Governing NEO-PACER Controls
Evidence acquisition follows the immutable-record doctrine and NEO-PACER identifier structure. Acquired material should be classified by evidence class, access level, provenance, chain of custody, authentication score, jurisdiction, and the FIN-021/022 confidence and recognition controls.

## Acquisition Priority
- `P0 CRITICAL` — evidence at immediate risk of loss, alteration, access termination, or limitation deadline.
- `P1 DISPOSITIVE` — primary evidence capable of resolving a central title, authority, custody, succession, or institutional-recognition issue.
- `P2 HIGH` — materially strengthens or contradicts a major claim.
- `P3 SUPPORTING` — useful context, chronology, identity, or corroboration.
- `P4 BACKGROUND` — non-dispositive historical/contextual material.

## Request Types
- `REQ-A` authenticated copy from issuing authority
- `REQ-B` certified court/registry record
- `REQ-C` institutional verification
- `REQ-D` account/custody/ledger verification where requester has lawful standing
- `REQ-E` archival/historical record
- `REQ-F` corporate/entity record
- `REQ-G` property/title record
- `REQ-H` audiovisual/source-file acquisition
- `REQ-I` internal governance/custody record
- `REQ-J` witness/declarant identification and statement

## Evidence Request Packet
Each request packet should contain:
1. NEO-PACER request ID.
2. Claim ID(s).
3. Exact record sought.
4. Date range.
5. Person/entity/asset identifiers.
6. Why the record is material.
7. Legal/public/contractual basis for requesting it.
8. Requested certification/authentication form.
9. Preservation request where appropriate.
10. Response deadline only where supported by applicable law, contract, rule, or forum authority.
11. Chain-of-custody intake instructions.

## Negative Response Handling
A response of "no record found" must preserve:
- exact institution searched
- repository/database searched
- date range
- identifier variants
- retention limitations
- search method if supplied
- whether the institution is the competent custodian

Absence from an incomplete or wrong repository is not dispositive negative evidence.
