## What we offer
A proposed modular, **noncustodial, read-only** platform that helps an authorized government organization account for and independently review digital assets and associated obligations. The current prototype is an evidence-oriented technology demonstration, not certified government infrastructure.

### Initial service package
1. **Reserve transparency interface:** agency-approved public wallet addresses, blockchain-height context, time-stamped balances, independent-explorer crosschecks, publication delays and limitations.
2. **Custodian reconciliation:** compare signed/certified custodian statements to observed balances; log exceptions and reconciliation state without treating observation as proof of ownership.
3. **Audit evidence:** cryptographic digest of read-only source response; explicit data provenance, reports for agency-appointed independent auditors and records-retention workflows.
4. **NEO identity/permissions:** proposed authenticated administrative workflows; no private-key capture or production custody assertions.
5. **Reporting support:** configurable statutory disclosures and public dashboards, separate from confidential agency operational and personal data.
6. **Bitcoin-native interoperability:** explorer, Counterparty asset metadata and read-only NOMNI/World Currency visibility **when independently verified**. Currency names or on-chain supply do not prove dollars, reserves, legal rights or eligible government investment status.

## Texas position
Texas Government Code §403.705 permits the Comptroller to contract with third parties for administration or management of the Texas Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. The §403.704 **$500 billion 24-month average market-cap** threshold governs cryptocurrency purchased with reserve money. Neither provision by itself authorizes the state to buy NOMNI, Orange Chip shares or private project credit tokens. Such purchases require separate legal authority.

Texas CMBL is a vendor discovery list, not an investment approval or contract award. The company reports vendor status, but a publicly verified matching CMBL profile / vendor number was not obtained in this review. TX-RAMP scope and level for hosted services must be determined with the purchasing agency. The 2026 Texas Reserve custody/liquidity solicitation is closed; do not present this statement as a response to it.
