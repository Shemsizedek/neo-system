# Noological Concubine Ethics Charter — draft
Status: internal ethical framework, not civil law.

1. **Equal human dignity.** Every adult retains full personhood and independent rights irrespective of title or relationship configuration.
2. **Voluntary association.** Relationships require freely given, specific, ongoing and revocable consent. Neither religious rank nor material support can compel intimacy.
3. **No ownership.** No person may be sold, assigned, inherited, pledged, tokenized, transferred, or treated as collateral.
4. **Independent agency.** Each participant controls their identity, movement, employment, health choices, personal property and lawful access to independent advice.
5. **Transparency.** Material household commitments and other relationships are disclosed as mutually agreed; deception is prohibited.
6. **Fair provision.** Shared expenses, dependents' care, housing and financial commitments must be documented without trapping a partner economically.
7. **Privacy.** No nonconsensual recording, publication, identity disclosure or circulation of intimate content.
8. **Non-retaliation.** Refusal, separation, complaints and departure must not trigger spiritual punishment, blacklisting or unlawful loss of essential support.
9. **Safeguarding.** No minors in adult relationship roles. Religious or educational authority must not be used to pressure students, employees, or dependents.
10. **Independent dispute process.** Provide confidential reporting, conflict-of-interest controls and access to civil authorities and professional assistance where needed.

**Terminology:** Ninsat is a modern NEO neologism for a voluntarily held adult relational/cultural title; it is not an attested ancient term and confers no ownership or legal marital status.
