# Temple Law of Marriage & Concubinage — proposed internal canon
Status: draft spiritual/organizational rules only. Not enacted Temple law, not civil family law, and not a claim of state-recognized marriage authority.

**Canon 1 — Dignity.** All participants are free adults with equal human dignity and independent civil rights.

**Canon 2 — Civil distinction.** A Temple blessing, vow, title, or covenant does not itself create a civil marriage or override applicable law. A civil marriage exists only as provided by the relevant jurisdiction.

**Canon 3 — Voluntary covenant.** Any religious household covenant must be freely entered and may be ended without coercion. No religious rank creates sexual entitlement.

**Canon 4 — Relationship plurality.** Temple teaching may discuss consensual polygyny and plural households; no ceremony or agreement shall be represented as legalizing otherwise prohibited civil conduct.

**Canon 5 — Ninsat office.** Ninsat is a modern project-defined cultural/relational designation for a consenting adult, not a civil marital status, proprietary interest, or historical attestation.

**Canon 6 — Stewardship and dependents.** Household leaders and partners share responsibility for transparency, fair provision and children's welfare consistent with applicable law.

**Canon 7 — Privacy and safeguarding.** Adult relationship data and media are confidential absent informed permission. Minors and people subject to exploitative authority dynamics are excluded from intimate institutional roles.

**Canon 8 — Property and instruments.** Property, trust, inheritance and token concepts such as SEBABOND and DOWRYNOTE require separate legal and financial analysis; no token grants rights over a person.

**Canon 9 — Complaints and due process.** Conflicts require impartial review, conflict-of-interest disclosure and protection against retaliation. Internal tribunals cannot extinguish access to civil courts or authorities.

**Canon 10 — Enactment.** Adoption requires designated Temple governance approval, recorded version, effective date, review of relevant jurisdictions, and a published amendment procedure. This draft has no binding effect until duly adopted.
