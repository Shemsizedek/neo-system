# Sacred Chambers Wife School — proposed adult curriculum
Status: NEO institutional proposal; not an accredited school or a live GISS LMS course.

## Purpose and eligibility
Voluntary adult education for prospective or current spouses and other interested adults, without a gender-exclusive obligation to attend. No marriage, housing, income, Temple membership, or intimacy may depend on enrollment or graduation. Keep this material outside minor-accessible GISD tracks.

## Six modules
1. **Noological Foundations:** distinguish NEO philosophy from documented history, psychology, and civil law.
2. **Autonomy and Partnership:** informed and revocable consent, reciprocal duties, independent finances, confidentiality, and exit rights.
3. **Household Stewardship:** cooperative budgeting, childcare planning, conflict resolution, division of labor, and equitable decision-making.
4. **Health and Wellbeing:** communication, boundaries, sexual-health literacy and referral to licensed clinicians where appropriate; no clinical claims for NEO-analysis.
5. **Cultural and Ceremonial Literacy:** Sacred Chambers of Nun, Het-Haru, Ninsat, and MSTA Adept Chamber as project-defined roles, not ancient lexical attestations.
6. **Law and Economic Literacy:** distinguish religious covenants, civil marriage, trusts, inheritance, and conceptual SEBABOND/DOWRYNOTE instruments; require jurisdiction-specific professional review.

## Assessment
Case studies, a reciprocal household charter, a privacy and consent exercise, and a family-budget scenario. Evaluation measures understanding, not obedience or personal relationship choices. No compulsory disclosure of sexual history.

## LMS controls
Adult-only access; opt-in enrollment; staff safeguarding review; no intimate data in public profiles; no automatic credentials, financial entitlements, or marital status; publish only after content and legal review.
