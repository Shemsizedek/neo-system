# Sacred Chambers Archetypes — creative identity register
Status: optional fictional/cultural identities; not mandatory social ranks or evidence of historical offices.

| Archetype | Cultural function | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Stewardess Priestess | Household stewardship, ritual hospitality, resource coordination | No authority over bodily autonomy or civil rights |
| Moon Maiden | Cycles, reflection, arts and contemplative practice | Adult-only if used in relationship context; not a youth designation |
| Het-Haru Steward | Care, beauty, mutual partnership and ceremonial hospitality | No automatic marriage or inheritance claim |
| Ninsat Companion | Voluntary adult companionship and creative partnership | Modern NEO title; no ownership, service obligation or compelled intimacy |
| Wisdom Keeper | Archival work, cultural interpretation and teaching | Cite historical sources; label NEO synthesis |
| Treasury Steward | Budgeting, savings and household transparency | No unilateral control of another adult's funds |

Archetypes are opt-in creative profiles. Participants may use none, one or multiple, and may withdraw them without penalty. No role should be represented as a legally recognized title or historically attested ancient office without evidence.
