# Sacred Relationship Protocols — Temple-based plural households
Status: voluntary adult relationship governance proposal.

1. **Entry:** confirm legal adulthood, independent choice, and freedom from coercive power relationships. No religious office, housing or employment is conditioned on romantic or sexual participation.
2. **Disclosure:** discuss existing relationships, expectations, children, privacy, sexual-health considerations and civil marital status.
3. **Mutual agreement:** document personal boundaries, decision-making, responsibilities, resources, dispute mechanisms and review intervals. Any intimate consent is specific and revocable and cannot be preauthorized by a contract.
4. **Household economics:** separate individual property from pooled budgets; clarify support for children and dependents; prohibit tokenization of people or intimacy.
5. **Ceremony:** optional spiritual recognition must not be described as conferring civil marriage, immigration, tax or inheritance status.
6. **Review:** offer private periodic check-ins, independent advocates and confidential safeguarding channels.
7. **Exit:** support safe departure, privacy, return of personal property and legally required family obligations. No retaliatory religious sanction.
8. **Records:** minimize sensitive data; restrict access, document consent to retention and allow appropriate correction/deletion consistent with law.

Legal review is mandatory before using these protocols in actual marriage, housing, employment, trust or financial transactions.
