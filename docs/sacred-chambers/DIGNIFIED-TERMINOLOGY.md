# Sacred Chambers — Dignified Terminology Standard
Status: editorial and doctrinal proposal.

## Preferred language
- **Ninsat Companion / Ninsat Steward:** optional modern NEO cultural/relational title for an adult secondary partner; clarify the individual's chosen identity.
- **Het-Haru Partner / Het-Haru Steward:** cultural titles within the wife-centered inner chamber; do not presume legal marriage.
- **Household Partner:** neutral public-language alternative where cultural terminology is unfamiliar.
- **Sacred Chambers Steward:** optional administrative or creative office, unrelated to intimate access.

## Retire in contemporary office descriptions
Avoid **slave-girl**, **property**, **owned woman**, **servant-wife**, and similar terms that imply a person is an asset or lacks autonomy. Avoid **mistress** as an imposed institutional label; it may be quoted only when discussing historical terminology or an individual's self-description.

## Editorial rule
Historical discussion may quote historically attested terminology with context, citations and clear distinction from modern norms. The project must not rewrite history by claiming its new terminology was used in antiquity.

## Implementation
Use the preferred terms in curriculum, Temple social-norm guidance, creator-brand briefs and Oracle/Noogle summaries. Do not silently change a person's self-selected title or legal identity. Obtain individual consent before public use.
