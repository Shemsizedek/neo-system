# NEO Social Content Lane Guard v0.1

## Purpose

Prevent publishing collisions between three distinct public-content products:

1. **Noocracy Papers** — the continuing World Bulletin series, also eligible for approved social distribution.
2. **Daily Noocracy Report** — social-media-only for now. It must not be published to the World Bulletin until the Noocracy Papers cycle is complete and the operator explicitly unlocks that destination.
3. **NOMNI / Ausarian Economics** — a separate social education/documentation lane based on the NOMNI Technical Paper, NOMNI Monetary Doctrine, and NOMNI Treasury Framework.

## Current destination matrix

| Lane | World Bulletin | Facebook | LinkedIn | YouTube Community |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Noocracy Papers | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Daily Noocracy Report | **No — locked** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| NOMNI / Ausarian Economics | No by default | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## NOMNI visual automation profile

Use the approved 16:9 dark futuristic trading-card interface:
- metallic navy / neon-cyan frame;
- top headline banner;
- upper architecture/system diagram;
- right-side **THE NOOCRATIC STEWARD** portrait;
- two lower data panels;
- conclusion footer;
- short labels, preferably six words or fewer;
- no blurry paragraph text inside generated images.

The long-form explanation belongs in the post caption. The image is a visual abstract, not the full article.

## Claim discipline

Keep these categories distinct:

- **Externally verifiable facts:** Bitcoin, Counterparty, on-chain asset metadata, and other claims supported by independent sources.
- **Issuer-defined framework:** treasury structure, redemption design, reserve rules, policy allocation, and institutional terminology.
- **Market calculations:** price, floor, XCP relationship, BTC-equivalent valuation, market capitalization, and related figures supplied by the operator or calculated from stated inputs.
- **Doctrine:** Ausarian Economics, Noological monetary design, and related normative principles.

Automation must not silently convert issuer-defined or doctrinal statements into independently established market or legal facts.

## Routing guard

The World Bulletin route for **Daily Noocracy Report** is explicitly disabled. A later change requires:
1. completion of the Noocracy Papers cycle;
2. explicit operator authorization; and
3. a versioned change to this routing policy.

This guard is additive and does not alter the existing Noocracy Papers publication workflow.
