Documented asset information

NOMNI is a world currency and unit of account on Bitcoin. The 1 NOMNI Bill is the one-unit bill designed for the same NOMNI token. Bill artwork and denomination records are linked to a SHA-256 hash that identifies the exact artwork; individual printed bills require separate funding and redemption verification.

This page republishes issuer-designated metadata from the NEO source repository. It is not evidence of current liquidity, reserves, redemption, wallet control, Bitcoin anchoring or regulatory status. On-chain issuance and transfers should be independently checked against the Bitcoin/Counterparty ledger.
