# NEO-PACER FIN-005 — Signatory & Authority Verification Matrix

## Case
TVMLSM666 v. NEOLPS999

## Purpose
FIN-005 tests each named signatory, witness, official, judge, banker, trustee, custodian, or purported international counterparty independently. A historically real officeholder does not authenticate a disputed instrument merely because the person's name, title, or signature appears on it.

## NEO evidentiary rule
For every signatory, distinguish:
1. identity;
2. office/capacity on the instrument date;
3. legal authority over the subject matter;
4. authentic signature/seal exemplar;
5. execution circumstances;
6. docket, registry, notarial, board, cabinet, bank, or archival entry;
7. counterparty acceptance;
8. later ratification, repudiation, modification, or judicial treatment.

No historical draft is altered. Conflicts are recorded as separate forensic events.

## Judicial control finding — Fred Ruiz Castro
Official Philippine Supreme Court records confirm Fred Ruiz Castro served as Chief Justice from January 5, 1976 through April 19, 1979. Accordingly, an instrument dated August 11, 1976 describing him as Chief Justice is chronologically compatible with his office. Chronological compatibility is only one authentication element and is not proof that he signed or that the Supreme Court issued the disputed instrument.

Required next evidence:
- Supreme Court docket number;
- original/certified order;
- rollo entry;
- promulgation record;
- Clerk of Court certification;
- division/en banc composition where applicable;
- signature exemplar comparison;
- service/notice record;
- subsequent appellate or enforcement treatment.

## Presidential signature control — Elpidio Quirino
Official Gazette instruments provide government-published Quirino signature blocks and presidential-form exemplars. These are useful comparison controls for typography, title, attestation, executive-secretary countersignature practice, date/location formula, and publication provenance. They do not establish that a separate claimant-side instrument bearing Quirino's name is genuine.

Required next evidence:
- Presidential issuance register;
- Malacañang/Official Gazette publication trail if applicable;
- Quirino papers and archival copy;
- countersignature/attestation records;
- notarial or treaty registration where asserted;
- counterpart institution copy.

## Banking and monetary officials
For every alleged central-bank, Treasury, Federal Reserve, BIS, PNB, BDO, UBS, Bank of China, National Bank of Dubai/Emirates NBD, or other bank signatory, FIN-005 requires:
- employment/appointment record on the date;
- delegated signing authority and monetary limits;
- board/committee authorization where required;
- specimen signature card or authenticated public exemplar;
- bank document-format comparison;
- transaction ledger or custody entry;
- receiving-counterparty confirmation.

## Signature comparison protocol
A visual resemblance alone is insufficient. NEO-PACER records:
- source and date of questioned signature;
- source and date of exemplar;
- original vs photocopy/scan status;
- resolution and evidence of alteration/compression;
- handwriting/forensic-document expert finding if obtained;
- seal/stamp characteristics;
- typewriter/font/printing technology compatibility;
- paper, ink and watermark testing where originals are available.

## Authority test
Even an authentic signature does not prove authority. Each instrument must separately pass:
- subject-matter authority;
- territorial/jurisdictional authority;
- monetary/delegation limits;
- required board/cabinet/court approval;
- required countersignature;
- filing/registration/publication requirements;
- legal capacity of the counterparty.
