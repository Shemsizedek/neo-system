# Shelton Estate & Co. — Virtual Bitcoin Investment Business Model

**Status:** Strategic draft; not an investment offering or approved financial forecast  
**Owner:** Shelton Estate & Co.

**As of:** 2026-10-16

## Executive summary and investor call to action

Shelton Estate & Co. proposes a virtual family-office-sponsored Bitcoin investment business. Subject to securities-law structuring, capital would be raised through eligible private tokenized investment offerings, with a majority of *deployable net proceeds* allocated to independently diligenced, third-party Bitcoin mining hash-power contracts. The firm would not own real estate or mining equipment for this strategy. The objective is to give eligible high-net-worth, ultra-high-net-worth and institutional investors a transparent, audited investment process with risk-controlled mining exposure.

**Call to action:** Qualified prospective investors may request the investment overview and a suitability/compliance review before receiving any offering documents. **No subscriptions, purchases or commitments are accepted through this draft.**

## Business and revenue model

- **Fundraising:** Private placement or other lawful issuance route, determined by counsel; Bitcoin/Counterparty-compatible tokenized interests only after legal, custody, transfer-restriction and investor-rights review.
- **Capital allocation policy (proposed):** 65–75% of net deployable proceeds to approved mining contracts; 10–15% liquidity/counterparty reserves; 5–10% compliance, custody and operations; 5–10% contingency and business development. Final allocations must reconcile to 100% and be approved per offering.
- **Mining economics:** Mining rewards less hash-power acquisition cost, pool fees, electricity/maintenance charges where applicable, network transaction fees, custody/FX costs and losses. Hash power does not confer guaranteed BTC production.
- **Firm economics:** Proposed disclosed management/administration fees and potential performance allocation subject to governing documents; investor capital and gross mined BTC are **not** automatically company revenue.
- **Government procurement:** Pursue authorized advisory, analytics, education or treasury-technology procurements through separate compliant contracts, not presumptive public-sector token sales.

## Five-year financial planning — methodology, not fabricated returns

**Target fundraising scenario:** $50 million in Year 1, explicitly **uncommitted and not raised**. The previously circulated example of 1,010 BTC mined from $50 million and $70.8 million Year-1 net profit is unsupported: dividing capital by an assumed cost per BTC is not a mining-production forecast. Do not use those numbers externally.

For each year 1–5, model separately:
1. Beginning BTC and fiat cash, investor subscriptions, fees and redemption/distribution liabilities.
2. Deployed hashrate (PH/s), delivery days and uptime.
3. Network hashrate/difficulty, BTC subsidy **including the expected 2028 halving**, transaction fees and pool economics.
4. BTC generated = expected share of network block rewards over uptime-weighted delivery, adjusted for fees.
5. Gross mining receipts = BTC produced × scenario BTC/USD realization price.
6. Vendor hash-power payments, pool/network fees, settlement/custody, legal and regulatory counsel, audits, security and technology, insurance, payroll/contractors, digital marketing, investor onboarding, government bid costs, taxes and contingency.
7. Fund-level net asset value and investor distributions **separately** from operating-company fee revenue, EBITDA and retained earnings.

Produce downside/base/upside scenarios (BTC price, difficulty, uptime, contract pricing, counterparty default, investor subscriptions). Do not declare profit or ROI until independently quotable vendor prices and a reproducible hash-production engine exist.

## Controls and integration

- **NEO CFO / STEWARD:** Firm versus family estate segregation; trustee approvals; concentration limits; monthly reporting.
- **NEO Books / LEDGER:** Investor-fund and management-company ledgers separated; vendor payments reconciled to BTC settlements.
- **NEO Crown Chain:** Proposed issuance/evidence registry; no live issuance until contract, legal classification, custody, sanctions and smart-contract audits.
- **NEO Pass / NEO Devices:** Authorized signatures, multi-factor approvals, access logging and document integrity.
- **NEOsync:** Confirm → review → merge → deploy gates; no automatic trading, subscription intake or transfers.
- **NEO FX / Treasury:** Read-only reference pricing and scenario analysis until authorized broker and execution workflows are certified.

5. Approve cybersecurity, smart-contract audit, KYC/AML, sanctions screening and accounting custody flow.
6. Review all public copy and paid ads before publication.
7. Obtain explicit authorized human **Confirm / Merge / Deploy** approvals.
