## 1. Neoteric Method

The Neoteric Method is the NEO philosophical method of inquiry. It synthesizes scientific inquiry, Socratic-style questioning, Nous/noetics, gnosis, Nuwaupu/Nuwau'bu, Nun and divine/natural intelligence.

Its three operating principles are:

1. **Law of Inquiry / Ma'at-Sekheru** — question assumptions; identify foundations, provenance and consequences; seek clarity rather than rhetorical victory.
2. **Dialectic of Duality / Neteru-Hekau** — examine opposed positions and seek coherent synthesis without forcing artificial agreement.
3. **Empirical Spirituality / Seshem-Heru** — test propositions through observable evidence, lived experience, disciplined reasoning and the corpus's spiritual/noological interpretive lens.

The corpus presents the Neoteric Method as an ancestral predecessor to later Greek dialectical traditions and describes the Socratic Method as a later canonized form. NEO Algo must retain this as a NEO historical/philosophical thesis unless independent evidence establishes a stronger evidence class.

## 2. Neoteric / Noocratic Probate

Probate replaces adversarial debate as the preferred NEO discourse protocol. Its objective is not victory over an opponent but the testing, clarification, reconciliation and transmission of propositions.

Operational maxim: **Debate seeks victory; Probate seeks verification and inheritance of resolved knowledge.**

Probate stages:

- Present the proposition and its provenance.
- Identify assumptions and material questions.
- Examine supporting and contrary evidence.
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, doctrine, experience and unresolved claim.
- Seek synthesis where evidence permits it.
- Record unresolved disagreement without manufacturing consensus.
- Produce a probated finding that may be taught, reconsidered or superseded as new evidence appears.

## 3. Noocratic Rules of Order

The World Professmentary / parliamentary model uses the following procedural vocabulary:

- **Motion / Motive** — present a matter to the Chambers.
- **Postpone** — defer consideration to a later time.
- **Amend** — modify a pending presentment.
- **Commit** — refer a presentment for study, inquiry or refinement.
- **Question** — pose a formal inquiry to the Chambers.
- **Table** — temporarily set aside a presentment without final disposition.
- **Adjourn** — conclude or suspend the sitting according to the applicable order.

The Rules of Order should implement probate rather than adversarial debate as the default deliberative model.
