# NEO-PACER FIN-004 — Instrument & Certificate Authenticity Matrix

**Case:** NEO-2026-GLOBAL-0001 — TVM-LSM-666 v. NEO-LPS-999

## Purpose
FIN-004 separates each alleged certificate, safekeeping receipt, gold guarantee, trust certificate, account identifier, custody receipt, and related instrument into an issuer-specific authentication track. No document is deemed authentic merely because it uses institutional names, logos, account codes, or financial terminology.

## Core authentication fields
For each instrument record:

1. Claimed issuer
2. Claimed date
3. Instrument title/type
4. Serial / reference / account code
5. Named owner / depositor / beneficiary
6. Signatories and titles
7. Seal / letterhead / security features
8. Governing law / institutional authority
9. Issuer format match
10. Archival register match
11. Ledger / custody match
12. Counterparty confirmation
13. Later renewals / transfers / substitutions
14. Duplicate / derivative instrument status
15. Current authentication disposition

## Federal Reserve control findings
Official Federal Reserve materials confirm that U.S. gold certificates are Treasury-issued instruments used within the Federal Reserve/Treasury monetary accounting structure, and that Federal Reserve Banks may hold custody items for Treasury, foreign central banks, foreign governments, depository institutions, and official international organizations. The Federal Reserve also states that private individuals cannot maintain Federal Reserve accounts, and private individuals or private-sector entities are not permitted to store gold in Federal Reserve vaults.

Accordingly, any claimed TVM-LSM-666 Federal Reserve instrument must establish the actual eligible account holder or official-sector intermediary. A document that merely labels a number as a personal "Federal Reserve account" fails the first institutional-capacity test.

## BIS control findings
The BIS states that it provides banking services to central banks and other official monetary/international institutions and does not accept deposits from private individuals or corporations. It also states that it does not certify, license, verify, or guarantee third-party financial products.

Accordingly, any alleged direct private BIS account, private BIS certificate, BIS guarantee, or BIS validation must identify an eligible official-sector intermediary and obtain BIS-side confirmation.

## Safekeeping Receipt / SKR doctrine
A safekeeping receipt is not automatically proof of ownership, value, or free-and-clear title to the underlying asset. FIN-004 therefore requires:

**receipt → custodian → vault/ledger entry → depositor identity → asset description → quantity/fineness → location → encumbrance status → release/transfer history.**

No SKR enters the verified asset total without the corresponding custody ledger.

## Gold Certificate doctrine
The phrase "gold certificate" is institution-specific. For U.S. Treasury/Federal Reserve purposes, gold certificates are statutory monetary-accounting instruments issued by Treasury to Reserve Banks; they are not ordinary private bullion ownership certificates. Other commercial-bank or central-bank certificates must be authenticated under that issuer's own historical format and authority.

## Current disposition
**FIN-004 — ACTIVE / UNRESOLVED.**

Documentary circulation and recurring nomenclature are established across the claimant corpus, but no instrument is elevated to VERIFIED solely by repetition. Issuer-side archival confirmation, authorized signatory verification, document-format comparison, and ledger/custody linkage remain mandatory.

## NEO rule
**Institutional name ≠ institutional authentication.**

Every issuing institution, including central banks, the Federal Reserve, BIS, Treasury, BSP/CBP, and commercial banks, remains subject to evidentiary scrutiny. Their records function as evidence, not as unquestionable adjudication.
