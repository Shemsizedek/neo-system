## Strategic thesis

NEO Digital Capital is the Bitcoin-native financial application and institutional infrastructure layer of the NEO System. Bitcoin is the reserve/settlement rail; Counterparty XCP supplies Bitcoin-native asset issuance and protocol functionality; NEO supplies the software, intelligence, accounting, underwriting, treasury, property, legal-document workflow, payments and market interfaces.

This document records NEO doctrine/product architecture. Legal characterizations, regulatory status, asset ownership, liquidity, valuation and market claims must be independently verified before external reliance.

### Orange Chip taxonomy

Orange Chip is the NEO category for eligible Bitcoin/Counterparty representations of documented economic interests. The token itself does not create the legal right; the underlying instrument and enforceable documentation define the right.

- Orange Equity — eligible equity/ownership interests
- Orange Interest — income, revenue, royalty or participation interests
- Orange Credit — debt, receivable, note or credit interests
- Extensions: Orange Realty, Fund, Commodity, Utility and Metal

Proposed lifecycle:
Asset -> Structure -> Underwrite -> Legal/Compliance -> Issue -> Account -> Wallet/Treasury -> Intelligence -> Eligible Market Infrastructure.

## World Monetary System (WMS)

WMS is a NEO-proposed monetary taxonomy and must be distinguished from official central-bank M0/M1/M2/etc. definitions.

- MB — World Monetary Base: Bitcoin / Mutual Credits
- M0 — Cryptographic Commercial Paper / Metallic Instrument
- M1 — XCP / XCPC Index: tokenized CES transaction accounts
- M2 — NMNI/NOMNI Index: tokenized CES trade/share/money-market accounts

## Institutional operating model

A prospective issuer enters through NEO Investments / Shelton Estate Capital. NEOwriter analyzes the asset; NEOlegals organizes structuring/document workflows; the Bitcoin/Counterparty layer handles eligible digital asset representation; NEObooks handles accounting; NEOpay handles wallet/treasury interactions; NEOscan handles intelligence/provenance; NEOhomes handles property operations when applicable; and NEOdex supplies market-interface infrastructure subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Cross-system integration

- NEO Algo: encode the WMS/Orange Chip classifications and lifecycle as machine-readable reasoning primitives; preserve fact/assumption/doctrine distinctions.
- NEO Oracle SI: valuation, risk, provenance, scenario analysis and verification across NEO Digital Capital.
- NEOsync: orchestrate module workflows and approvals; consequential issuance/legal/market actions remain gated.
- NEO Router: route asset, accounting, legal, treasury, market and educational requests to the appropriate module.
- NEO Crawler: collect authorized/public issuer, asset, market and provenance data for NEOscan/Oracle.
- NEO Lingo/Lexicon: canonicalize Orange Chip, WMS and module terminology.
- GISS / NEO LMS: teach Bitcoin, Counterparty, mutual credit, WMS, RWA structuring, accounting, underwriting and compliance with clear separation between doctrine and established external facts.
- NEO Law: maintain authorization, jurisdiction, securities/market, custody, banking, privacy, KYC/AML and counsel-review gates. Do not infer that tokenization itself establishes ownership, regulatory compliance or liquidity.
- Internal NEO Society: support informed consent, transparency, provenance, non-deceptive representation, privacy, user-controlled assets/keys where feasible, and no misrepresentation of licensing or guaranteed returns.
- N.I.A. / Project 144: institutional intelligence, due diligence and provenance workflows within explicit authorization boundaries.
