# Project 144 — Counterpay Internal Rail / NEO eGaaS

## Scope of this staging gate

This gate adds an authenticated **offline-only record commitment preparation endpoint** to the existing NEO eGaaS API. It reuses `prepareEgaasAnchor` from the existing eGaaS anchor work (PR #1346). It does not create a new blockchain, pay an agent, issue an asset, or broadcast.

### Data contract

`POST /api/v1/egaas/internal-rail/prepare` is **disabled by default** through the injected `counterpayInternalRailEnabled=false` option and uses the trusted NEOpass subject resolver. It accepts `{entries:[{recordRef,recordClass,privacy,commitment}]}` where:
- `recordRef` is an opaque random UUIDv4 generated independently of original records and identifiers.
- `recordClass` is one of IDENTITY, COURT_FILING, CALL_LOG, PROPERTY, MEDICAL, GOVERNMENT_REGISTRATION, AGENT_WORK, FINANCIAL_INTENT, EDUCATION or GENERAL.
- `privacy` is RESTRICTED or HIGHLY_RESTRICTED (high sensitivity classes require HIGHLY_RESTRICTED).
- `commitment` is a unique **pre-salted**, domain-separated 32-byte SHA-256 commitment in lowercase hex, generated by a trusted encrypted custody service. Do **not** submit a direct SHA-256 of a low-entropy identifier or record; dictionary attacks could identify it.

Inputs are strict (no extra fields, no raw record contents or personal identifiers), max 128 entries and 64 KiB request. The response contains a prepared root, candidate Counterparty message, and off-chain membership proofs. No keys, invoices, transaction data, funds, Bitcoin network calls, persistent storage or privileges are created.

### Status and proof boundaries

The endpoint always returns `state=prepared_offchain`, `anchoring.verified=false`, `transactionId=null`, and `operations.broadcast=false`. A prepared root is **not** a Bitcoin timestamp, confirmed record, legal filing or evidence of government registration. Valid proof of inclusion later requires independent Bitcoin and Counterparty verification, correct network and confirmations, a verified payload binding, and applicable institutional authority. Bitcoin block time is not an exact event time. Do not put medical, court, property, identity, phone-call or private data on-chain; ideally keep even public record categories off-chain until privacy assessment.

### NeoMask, NEO Crown Chain, agents and BVM/NVM

- **NEOpass** authenticates humans/services; it does not prove control of a Bitcoin address by itself.
- **NeoMask** remains the sole intended signer bridge, with origin grants, NEO Guardian review, simulations, and explicit approval. This gate does not call it or access signing keys.
- **NEO Crown Chain** serves as the application registry and verification layer for public Bitcoin/Counterparty evidence, **not** a replacement consensus network.
- **Agents/bots:** reuse operator-controlled public-address proposals (PR #1380). Require signed challenge, revocation/rotation, scoped budgets, accounting, and proof of transaction confirmation before payment. Unverified proposals are not deposit addresses.
- **BVM**: future Bitcoin-anchored deterministic verification / rule execution using off-chain execution with committed inputs and outputs; **NVM**: NEO application policy/runtime and workflows. Neither is live here or a new Bitcoin consensus feature.

### Compliance and privacy

- This is an internal institutional evidence system, not a statutory identity authority, government registry, court of record or land registry. Record acceptance, title effect, legal recognition, due process, retention, and licensing depend on competent external institutions and applicable law.
- Medical records require access restrictions, consent/legal basis, encryption, retention controls, breach response, and applicable HIPAA or other privacy compliance. Private records never leave encrypted custody in this gate.
- Agent-to-agent transfers must not create artificial trade volume; keep internal transfers transparent and separate from genuine third-party market activity.
- Prefer batching many salted commitments into one eventual anchor. Unique small-value Bitcoin outputs are **not required** for each record and may be uneconomic, traceable, dust-like or spam-like.
