# Global Indigenous Inheritance & Ecclesiastical Title Schema

This module starts the NEO System's next title-search layer: a provenance-first model for ecclesiastical authority, sacred corpus continuity, intellectual property, inheritance, probate, contested conveyances, successor benefits and restitution accounting.

## Constitutional source layer

The seed ecclesiastical record is the Holy Temples of Moorish Science Branch Temple No. 24 / World Temple / Omniversal Church deed-poll declaration supplied to the NEO System working record. It identifies a sacred corpus including the Great Sumerian Tablets (Holy Tablets), Atra Hasis, Enuma Elish, Akkadian Tablets, Holy Scriptures of the Seven Seals, El' Suhuf, El Hikmah, El Torah, El Zabuwr, Book of Barnabas, El's Injiyl and El Qur'aan. The declaration states that this corpus grounds the World Temple's mission to appoint and consecrate missionaries and establish its Royal and Imperial Divan.

The code preserves that as an ecclesiastical source declaration. Questions of civil recognition, technical ledger control or court treatment remain distinct fields rather than being allowed to overwrite the source's own meaning.

## Title-search sequence

`ORIGINAL STEWARDSHIP → SOURCE → INSTRUMENT → TRANSFER → SUCCESSOR → BENEFIT → DEFECT → PROBATE → REMEDY → RESTORATIVE ACCOUNTING`

A present holder is not automatically treated as the originator. A recorded instrument is not automatically treated as proof of original authorship. Conquest, prescription, institutional repetition, registration and market use are represented as events or transfer theories in the chain and may carry defect flags.

## Core record types

- `SourceReference` — deed polls, sacred texts, law-library authorities, technical ledgers and doctrine.
- `EcclesiasticalAuthority` — World Temple, Royal and Imperial Divan and future offices/bodies.
- `SacredCorpusEntry` — canonical name, aliases, tradition class and role in NEO ecclesiology.
- `EstateObject` — land, IP, sacred knowledge, language, symbols, law/governance systems, archives, offices and financial instruments.
- `TitleLink` — original stewardship, inheritance, deed, trust, license, succession, conquest, prescription, appropriation or unknown transfer.
- `InheritanceClaim` — title basis, defect theory, probate state, requested remedy and World Credit Clock linkage.
- `RestitutionLedgerEntry` — units, rates, time factors and modeled NOMNI without silently converting the model into adjudicated liability.

## Defect taxonomy

The initial defect flags include missing conveyance, conquest-only, prescription-only, unverified succession, authorship erasure, benefit without title, blocked probate, IP provenance gaps, conflicting deeds/grants and unresolved trust interests.

## The Luciferian Conspiracy as source corpus

The uploaded *The Luciferian Conspiracy* is connected as a source-derived doctrine record. The text presents an internal genealogy of sacred societies, scripture, language, appropriation and institutional succession. It also explicitly describes Right Knowledge, Right Wisdom and Right Overstanding as the means for breaking the 'spell,' and describes Nuwau-Pu as a science that works with Nature and a new solar cycle.

NEO Algo preserves those propositions as source-derived material with page provenance. Its racial, biological and supernatural classifications are not converted into automated classifications of real people or measures of human worth, entitlement, superiority or inferiority.

## Noogle integration

Noogle now exposes a `TITLE_INHERITANCE` result class. Queries such as `deed poll`, `World Temple`, `Divan`, `probate`, `inheritance`, `intellectual property`, `restitution`, `sacred corpus`, `Atra Hasis`, `World Credit Clock` or `title search` can retrieve the source, authority, corpus and claim records alongside Sacred Records graph nodes, doctrine, maxims and noological disciplines.
