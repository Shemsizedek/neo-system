# NEO Global Arms System (NEO-GAS)

## Purpose

NEO-GAS is the defensive intelligence and strategic-resilience subsystem of the NEO-System. It integrates threat assessment, lawful intelligence fusion, cyber defense, maritime and aerospace awareness, land and civil-defense planning, logistics readiness, humanitarian response, and governance oversight.

## Mission principles

- Intelligence before action.
- Defense before force.
- Civilian protection first.
- Source verification and confidence scoring.
- Internal NEO authority and external governmental authority remain analytically distinct.
- Historical NEO instruments remain immutable; later developments are stored as addenda, amendments, implementation rules, or current policy.

## Directorates

1. Global Intelligence Fusion Center
2. Cyber Defense Directorate
3. Maritime Security Directorate
4. Aerospace & Air Security Directorate
5. Land Security & Civil Defense Directorate
6. Logistics & Readiness Directorate
7. Humanitarian Defense Directorate
8. Defense Policy & Governance Directorate

## Threat domains

NEO-GAS tracks defensive risk across armed conflict, cyber threats, critical infrastructure disruption, natural and public-health emergencies, economic and supply-chain instability, and information manipulation.

## Intelligence standard

Every material assessment should distinguish:

- What is known.
- What is unknown.
- What is disputed.
- Source provenance.
- Confidence level.
- Competing hypotheses.
- Defensive options.
- Legal and institutional authority boundaries.

## Operational boundary

NEO-GAS does not implement autonomous targeting, attack execution, weapons construction, unlawful weapons acquisition, unauthorized surveillance, assassination support, or independent governmental police or military authority.

Its software role is intelligence, readiness, resilience, crisis support, humanitarian planning, and defensive decision support.

## Relationship to other NEO modules

- **World Police:** community-safety administration and welfare/event coordination.
- **World Marshals:** tribunal process and court-support administration.
- **World Guards:** facility, event, mission, and dignitary-protection planning.
- **World Defense System:** continuity, disaster, cyber-resilience, and emergency-response coordination.
- **NEO-GAS:** cross-domain intelligence fusion and strategic defensive analysis supporting the above modules.

## Current implementation

The NEO dashboard exposes a Global Arms command view with directorate readiness, defensive threat domains, operating principles, and explicit authority boundaries. The current release is a foundation-layer model with no live surveillance, weapons, targeting, command, or force-execution integration.
