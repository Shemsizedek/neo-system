## 1. Claim under examination
Claimant-side publications identify a **Last Will and Testament dated 7 April 1962**. King Solomon United Tribal Republic Trust describes it as an instrument governing alleged succession from the **KORAN–FLAT–CRUZ, VILLAMOR, TALLANO family clan and Trustee Depositor** in favor of **TVM-LSM-666**.

This dossier records that assertion as an investigative lead. It does **not** treat the publication itself as proof that the will was validly executed, admitted to probate, effective against particular property, or capable of transferring assets held by banks or third parties.

## 2. Documentary continuity
The 1962 instrument is not an isolated allegation in the claimant ecosystem. Later Alpha Omega material uses the same or related identifiers — KORAN, FLAT, TVM-LSM-666 and 010-22-74-O-A — and links them to alleged trust deposits, bullion holdings, certificates, and later authentication instruments.

That continuity is probative of **claim persistence and internal documentary architecture**. It is not independent issuer authentication.

## 3. Chain-of-title question
AUTH-004 must determine whether the alleged 1962 instrument legally continues, modifies, supplements, supersedes, or revokes AUTH-003 (the asserted 11 August 1952 will).

The required comparison is clause-by-clause:
1. testator identity and legal capacity;
2. beneficiary/heir identity;
3. KORAN / FLAT / TVM-LSM-666 identity bridge;
4. property specifically devised;
5. residuary estate language;
6. trust/account schedules incorporated by reference;
7. executor/trustee appointments;
8. revocation of prior wills;
9. governing-law clauses;
10. signatures, witnesses, attestation, acknowledgment and page formalities;
11. probate or other judicial allowance;
12. subsequent codicils, judgments, settlements or confirmations.

## 4. Asset-title rule
A testamentary instrument can transmit only interests legally owned by the decedent and legally transmissible at death. Therefore even an authenticated 1962 will would not, by itself, authenticate:
- OCT T-01-4;
- bullion quantities;
- bank deposits;
- KORAN/FLAT account codes;
- authority over central-bank, BIS, IMF, World Bank or private-bank property;
- later global-funder or diplomatic designations.

Each asset requires its own provenance and custody chain.

## 5. Forensic authentication gates
### Gate A — Original instrument
Obtain the complete original or a certified archival/probate copy, including cover, schedules, seals, endorsements and reverse sides.

### Gate B — Execution
Authenticate signatures, witnesses, notary/notarial register, date, place, pagination, alterations and physical characteristics.

### Gate C — Testamentary capacity and identity
Establish the testator's civil identity, capacity, relationship to the alleged estate and authority over each devised asset.

### Gate D — Probate
Locate the probate petition, docket number, court, notice/publication, testimony, order allowing or disallowing the will, letters testamentary/administration and estate inventory.

### Gate E — 1952→1962 succession bridge
Determine whether the 1962 instrument expressly references the 1952 will and whether it revokes or changes prior dispositions.

### Gate F — KORAN/FLAT nomenclature
Trace the earliest independently authenticated appearance of each identifier. A later document repeating a code does not prove that the code existed in 1962.

### Gate G — Bank and bullion linkage
For every account or bullion claim, obtain bank-side records: opening documents, signature cards, ledger entries, custody receipts, assays, serial/bar lists, transfers, correspondent confirmations, audit records and lawful disclosure/authentication.
