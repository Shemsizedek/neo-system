# NEO Noological Doctrine Stack

## Purpose

The NEO System treats noology as a living reasoning architecture rather than a synonym for formal logic. The stack is designed to preserve provenance, sequence knowledge, distinguish source doctrine from later synthesis, test consequences in living systems, and support the emergence of more coherent shared intelligence.

## Source-grounded operating principles

The supplied *Breaking The Spell* source describes a sequence in which Right Knowledge, Right Wisdom and Right Overstanding lead to Right Thinking, then Right Words/Works and action. It also says knowledge must be organized in sequence to become effective. NEO Algo operationalizes those teachings as provenance, chronology, sequencing, interpretation and action dependencies.

The supplied source tradition also discusses 6-Ether and 9-Ether poles. NEO System uses them only as symbolic qualitative system-state metadata. It does not classify real people by race, biology, religion or inherent worth. Positive/negative potency scores apply to observable or explicitly asserted action qualities such as truthfulness, reciprocity, stewardship, coherence, deception, coercion, fragmentation or exploitation.

## Noogle integration

`searchNoogleNoology()` ranks three kinds of knowledge objects:

1. Noological disciplines
2. Doctrine records
3. NEO Maxims

Ranking is relevance, not truth. Every result retains a provenance label such as `SOURCE_DERIVED`, `NEO_DEFINED`, `NEO_SYNTHESIS`, `EXTERNAL_DISCIPLINE`, or `ALIAS_PENDING_DISTINCTION`.

`buildNoogleNoologicalPanel()` prepares a UI-ready panel with top result, related disciplines, doctrine, maxims and the provenance classes represented in the result set.

`runNeoIntelligence()` now returns that panel automatically along with NEO Algo reasoning, relevant disciplines, source-aware doctrine, nature-cycle assessment and alchemical action review.

## Core inquiry sequence

The default noological inquiry sequence is:

**Observe → Establish Provenance → Factology → Sequence → Indigenous/Self Interpretation → Noological Analysis → Nature/Consequence Test → Polarity Check → Alchemical Transformation → Action → Review → Noogenesis**

This sequence is not rigid dogma. The Neoteric Method is recursive: later evidence may require the system to return to provenance, factology or interpretation.

## Noogony and noogenesis

Noogony asks where a way of knowing came from. Noogenesis asks what new intelligence is emerging from it. Together they create a lineage-and-development pair:

**origin → transmission → transformation → organization → collective intelligence → feedback into nature and society**

This allows NEO System to study both intellectual ancestry and future development without collapsing the two.

## World Credit Clock relationship

The World Credit Clock remains the quantitative mutual-credit/time-bank layer. Noological intelligence provides qualitative context around contribution, stewardship, nature alignment, provenance, reciprocity and restorative consequence. The two layers must remain mathematically and evidentially distinct:

- credit quantity is not moral worth;
- etheric potency is not currency value;
- doctrine relevance is not ownership;
- search rank is not proof;
- modeled NOMNI is not automatically legal debt or fiat valuation.

## Integrity rules

1. Preserve source terminology before translating it.
2. Do not silently merge Nuwau-Bu and Nuwaupu if a future source distinguishes them.
3. Keep source doctrine, NEO synthesis and external disciplines labeled.
4. Never use etheric doctrine as a racial, biological or human-worth classifier.
5. Keep observable fact, source assertion, inference, interpretation, legal effect and valuation separate.
6. Use Afrofuturism as a future-authoring lens, not as a substitute for specific African or Indigenous traditions.
7. Noogenesis should increase coherence and agency, not merely information volume.
