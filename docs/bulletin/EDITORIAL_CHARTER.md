# NEO Bulletin / World Bulletin — Gate 1 editorial charter
Status: PROPOSED; draft-first. Owner: World Temple editorial office. Supporting agent: NEO Marketer. Date: 2026-11-09.

## Purpose
A publicly accessible, source-led series of civic lessons, public finance and economics education, legal literacy, comparative philosophy, scriptural commentary, historical research, community dialogue and precise responses to published claims. This is a new lane; preserve World Temple Letter Bulletins, Noocracy Papers, Neoteric Papers, and existing social-lane locks.

## Evidence policy
Separate: primary-source fact; secondary reporting; inference; opinion; religious doctrine/interpretation; allegation; unverified claim; legal finding. An accusation about a person or organization is not a fact without evidence; absence of proof is not proof of a cover-up. Document source title, original URL, author, publication/event date, archive locator, direct quotations (respect copyrights), evidence for and against, confidence, reviewer, and date checked. Link to corrections and preserve superseded versions.

Never claim that private religious/civic organizations confer immunity from generally applicable law. Do not describe legal filings as charges filed unless a government docket confirms it. No threats, dogpiling, repeated unwanted targeting, or personal information disclosure. Scrutinize claims about Epstein records, wrongdoing by critics, or convictions with contemporaneous primary documentation, and obtain editorial/legal review before publication.

## Standard article template
Title; date; series; thesis; scope; source/claim table; evidence and counterevidence; evidence classification; analysis; religious interpretation (if relevant); practical civic lessons; limitations and open questions; right of reply; corrections policy; sources and archive IDs; review/approval log.

## Automation contract
Intake -> source capture -> source verification -> opposing evidence -> editorial draft -> fact/legal/rights review (for disputed people/organizations) -> human approval -> WordPress publication -> NEO Marketer distribution -> URL/receipt/uptime checks -> public corrections.
No auto-publishing of personal allegations, disputed criminal claims, harassment accusations, or new legal conclusions. First gate runs as draft-only. Existing World Bulletin routing locks stay intact.
