# NEO-PACER FIN-032 — Burden of Proof, Evidentiary Weight & Standard-of-Review Engine

## Purpose
FIN-032 defines how much evidentiary support is required before a proposition can move from allegation or investigative lead into an internal administrative finding, title conclusion, treasury exception, recovery demand, adjudicative determination, or referral to an external competent authority.

The engine is designed to prevent two opposite errors:
1. promoting weak or derivative material into findings merely because it is voluminous or repeated; and
2. dismissing unconventional evidence merely because it is unfamiliar, politically sensitive, ecclesiastical, dynastic, or outside ordinary institutional narratives.

## Core Separation Rule
The following states must remain distinct:
- raw information
- allegation
- investigative lead
- inference
- authenticated artifact
- independently corroborated fact
- disputed fact
- internal administrative finding
- internal adjudicative finding
- external adjudication
- unknown/unresolved

No system component may silently promote one state into another.

## Burden Classes

### BP-0 — Intake Threshold
Use for raw submissions, social-media material, witness statements, claimant documents, tips, archived media, and public allegations.
Requirement: identifiable proposition + source + provenance sufficient to preserve the lead.
Result: may enter investigation; cannot support an adverse finding by itself.

### BP-1 — Reasonable Investigative Basis
Use to justify opening or expanding an investigation.
Requirement: articulable facts or evidence that make the proposition sufficiently plausible to test.
Result: permits targeted evidence acquisition, preservation, interviews, registry research, and contradiction testing.

### BP-2 — Preponderance / More Likely Than Not
Default internal civil/administrative fact standard unless a governing instrument requires otherwise.
Requirement: total reliable evidence makes the proposition more probable than its contrary.
Result: may support ordinary internal findings, accounting determinations, non-punitive governance decisions, and civil-style claim analysis.

### BP-3 — Clear and Convincing Evidence
Use where the consequence is materially adverse, difficult to reverse, reputationally serious, title-sensitive, or dependent on contested authenticity/succession.
Requirement: evidence produces a firm belief or high probability that the proposition is true.
Result: required by default for serious internal misconduct findings, contested succession/title findings, fraud-type internal determinations, and extraordinary recovery assertions unless stricter law applies.

### BP-4 — Beyond Reasonable Doubt / Criminal Standard
Reserved for competent criminal adjudication where applicable law uses this standard.
NEO-PACER, internal tribunals, NEOsync, or NEO Law must not represent an internal conclusion as an external criminal conviction merely because evidence appears strong.

### BP-X — Governing-Instrument / External-Law Standard
Where a statute, contract, arbitration clause, probate code, registry rule, court rule, treaty, ecclesiastical constitution, or other controlling instrument specifies a different burden, record and apply that standard within its valid jurisdiction.

## Evidentiary Weight Model
Evidence weight is multi-factor and not based on document count alone.

Each item is evaluated for:
- provenance
- authenticity
- source independence
- contemporaneity
- directness
- competence of declarant/issuer
- chain of custody
- internal consistency
- external corroboration
- institutional/registry recognition
- contradiction burden
- motive/bias where relevant
- completeness
- asset/person/event specificity

## Weight Classes
- `EW-0 NO SUBSTANTIVE WEIGHT` — rumor, unidentified repost, unsupported assertion
- `EW-1 LEAD WEIGHT` — identifiable but uncorroborated source
- `EW-2 SUPPORTING WEIGHT` — traceable evidence with partial corroboration
- `EW-3 MATERIAL WEIGHT` — authenticated or strongly corroborated evidence materially probative of the proposition
- `EW-4 HIGH WEIGHT` — primary, contemporaneous, authenticated evidence with strong contextual consistency
- `EW-5 DISPOSITIVE/NEAR-DISPOSITIVE WEIGHT` — competent primary record plus relevant registry, court, custodian, issuer, or counterparty confirmation where such confirmation is logically expected

`EW-5` does not mean infallible; contrary competent evidence must still be considered.

## Independence Rule
Ten derivative websites repeating one originating assertion do not equal ten independent confirmations.

Use FIN-021 independence classes to distinguish:
- common-source repetition
- derivative republication
- coordinated/affiliated publication
- genuinely independent corroboration

## Authentication Rule
A document can be authentic and still contain:
- mistaken factual assertions
- advocacy
- hearsay
- legal conclusions
- disputed ownership claims
- later-invalidated decisions

Therefore:
`Document Authenticity ≠ Proposition Truth ≠ Legal Effect ≠ Counterparty Recognition`

## Evidence Hierarchy — General
The following hierarchy is presumptive, not absolute:
1. competent primary record from issuing authority/registry/court/custodian
2. authenticated contemporaneous original or certified copy
3. independent contemporaneous records
4. authenticated first-person testimony with direct knowledge
5. reliable institutional/business records
6. expert analysis based on disclosed methodology
7. derivative records and later summaries
8. advocacy materials
9. social-media claims and anonymous allegations

Context can change weight; no source is immune from contradiction testing.

## Adverse Finding Gate
Before an adverse internal finding against a person or entity:
- FIN-030 must establish internal jurisdiction or clearly label the conclusion as non-binding investigative analysis
- FIN-031 must establish adequate notice and opportunity to respond where fairness requires it
- material evidence must be disclosed subject to lawful confidentiality/sealing rules
- exculpatory and contradictory evidence must be preserved and weighed
- burden and standard must be identified in writing
- unresolved material uncertainty must be stated

## Title / Succession Standard
A title or succession proposition cannot reach a strong internal finding solely because:
- a genealogy is long
- a claimant possesses many documents
- a document uses royal/ecclesiastical titles
- a filing exists
- a UCC record was accepted for filing
- a source repeats historic claims

Use FIN-029 chain continuity and require the burden appropriate to the requested conclusion. For externally enforceable modern title, competent registry/court/counterparty evidence remains necessary where the governing system requires it.

## Treasury / Financial Exception Standard
Treasury exceptions under FIN-019 may be classified before misconduct is proven.

Distinguish:
- accounting anomaly
- control failure
- unauthorized transaction indicator
- negligence allegation
- intentional misconduct allegation
- fraud allegation
- externally adjudicated wrongdoing

An unexplained transaction can justify investigation without proving fraudulent intent.

## Fraud / Sabotage / Conspiracy Allegations
Because these allegations are especially serious:
- use neutral allegation language until the applicable burden is met
- identify each element separately
- distinguish agreement, intent, overt conduct, causation, and damages
- do not infer coordination solely from association, symbolism, common membership, or temporal proximity
- preserve direct, circumstantial, exculpatory, and contradictory evidence
- internal conclusions do not substitute for criminal adjudication by competent authority

## Social Media Evidence
Social media can carry evidentiary weight when authenticated.

Record:
- account identity
- original vs repost
- timestamp
- URL/content ID
- archive capture
- edits/deletion status
- media metadata when available
- quoted vs original statements
- direct knowledge vs repetition
- relationship between accounts

A post may prove that a statement was made without proving the statement itself is true.

## Witness / Declarant Weight
Evaluate:
- personal knowledge
- role/access
- contemporaneity
- consistency
- corroboration
- bias/conflict
- prior inconsistent statements
- recantation history
- documentary support
- opportunity to perceive

Recantation is a new evidentiary event; it does not silently erase the original statement.

## Standards of Review
FIN-032 defines review posture for later tribunal/appellate layers:

### SR-1 De Novo
Reconsider legal interpretation, jurisdiction, or mixed questions without deference where the reviewing body has authority.

### SR-2 Clear Error
Use for factual findings where the original factfinder had a superior evidentiary position.

### SR-3 Abuse of Discretion
Use for discretionary internal procedural or administrative decisions.

### SR-4 Substantial Evidence
Use where the issue is whether enough reliable evidence exists in the whole record to support an administrative conclusion.

### SR-5 Governing-Instrument Standard
Use the review standard expressly established by applicable constitution, bylaws, arbitration agreement, statute, rule, or controlling order.

## Burden-Shifting
Burden shifting is permitted only where logically or legally justified.
Examples:
- a claimant bears initial burden to establish title/succession
- production of an authenticated instrument may shift a production burden to a challenger, but not automatically the ultimate burden of persuasion
- a respondent's silence after valid notice does not convert an unsupported allegation into proven fact
- failure to produce uniquely controlled records may support a carefully limited adverse inference only where applicable rules permit and surrounding facts justify it

## Negative Evidence
Absence of a record is weighted according to:
- completeness of repository
- retention policies
- date range searched
- naming/indexing variations
- successor institutions
- disaster/loss history
- legal restrictions
- whether the record would ordinarily be expected to exist

`No record found` is not automatically `event did not occur`.

## Standard Selection Matrix
Default internal standards:
- investigation opening: `BP-1`
- ordinary accounting exception: `BP-1/BP-2` depending consequence
- routine governance fact: `BP-2`
- serious misconduct finding: `BP-3`
- contested title/succession finding: `BP-3` plus FIN-029 continuity
- external recovery demand: enough authenticated evidence to satisfy FIN-023 readiness and the recipient forum's standard
- criminal accusation presented as conviction: prohibited absent competent external adjudication

## Decision Record
Every material finding must state:
- proposition
- burden applied
- evidence supporting
- evidence opposing
- material excluded or given low weight and why
- authentication status
- source-independence status
- jurisdiction status
- due-process status
- finding
- confidence
- unresolved issues
- review standard
- appeal/reconsideration path if applicable
