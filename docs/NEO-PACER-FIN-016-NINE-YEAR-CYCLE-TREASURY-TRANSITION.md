# NEO-PACER FIN-016 — Nine-Year Cycle & Treasury Transition Audit

## Source basis
This module distinguishes the Noone Society Affidavit of Organization from the later Omniversal Church Covenant.

## Foundational dates appearing in the Noone Society Affidavit
- 11 October 2015: Security Agreement entered on the 11th day of the 10th month, Common Era 2015.
- 18 October 2015: page 1 states "2015 Oct, 18" in the affidavit heading and says Noone Society is hereby established.
- 18 November 2015: page 2 states that Noone Society was recorded in Harris County / Texas on the 18th day of November 2015; the Hold Harmless and Indemnity also bears 11/18/2015.

The affidavit itself does not contain an express "nine-year cycle" provision.

## Nine-year governance provision
The express nine-year election language appears in Article 9.02 of the Omniversal Church Covenant. It provides that listed World Temple officers hold office until the "nine year election for a new Chancellor," unless removed under the bylaws or upon death. Other World Ministry offices may have one-to-three-year terms, and Ministerial Directors generally have one-year terms.

## Cycle calculation
Because the Covenant does not, in the currently reviewed text, expressly state that the first nine-year cycle begins on a specific 2015 affidavit date, the exact cycle anchor remains an interpretive issue.

If the organizational-establishment date of 18 October 2015 is used as the anchor:
- Cycle 1: 18 Oct 2015 – 17 Oct 2024
- Cycle 2: 18 Oct 2024 – 17 Oct 2033
- Cycle 3: 18 Oct 2033 – 17 Oct 2042
- Cycle 4: 18 Oct 2042 – 17 Oct 2051

If the recorded/public-record date of 18 November 2015 is used:
- Cycle 1: 18 Nov 2015 – 17 Nov 2024
- Cycle 2: 18 Nov 2024 – 17 Nov 2033
- Cycle 3: 18 Nov 2033 – 17 Nov 2042
- Cycle 4: 18 Nov 2042 – 17 Nov 2051

If the 11 October 2015 Security Agreement date is used:
- Cycle 1: 11 Oct 2015 – 10 Oct 2024
- Cycle 2: 11 Oct 2024 – 10 Oct 2033
- Cycle 3: 11 Oct 2033 – 10 Oct 2042
- Cycle 4: 11 Oct 2042 – 10 Oct 2051

## Current finding
Under every plausible 2015 anchor identified in the source record, the second nine-year period began in 2024. Therefore, by 2026 the organization is within the second nine-year period if the cycle is calculated from the 2015 founding/recording instruments.

## Governance caution
The source record does not yet establish which of the three 2015 dates legally controls the nine-year Chancellor election cycle. That must be resolved by the earliest election resolution, board minutes, amendment, installation record, or other governance instrument expressly defining the first Chancellor term.

## Treasury transition control
The World Treasurer is an officer whose authority is governed by the Covenant. Removal, replacement, access revocation, records handoff, account reconciliation, and preservation of prior records should be separately documented. Alleged misconduct or sabotage must remain a separate evidentiary issue from the fact of removal.
