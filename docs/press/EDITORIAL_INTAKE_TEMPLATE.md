## Federal FOIA or North Carolina public-records request

To the records custodian:

I request copies of the following existing records: [record types, subject, date range, known identifiers]. I prefer an electronic response where available. Please identify the basis for any withholding and provide segregable nonexempt portions. Contact me before costs exceed [amount].

Federal executive-agency records: https://www.foia.gov/how-to.html
North Carolina state/local records: https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_132/GS_132-6.html
Court docket and sealed-record requests require their own access procedure.

## Prepublication release checklist
- [ ] Primary evidence checked and dated
- [ ] Allegations distinguished from court findings and confirmed facts
- [ ] Counterevidence included and source independence verified
- [ ] Opportunity for response where appropriate
- [ ] Accuracy, legal risk, safety, privacy, and confidential-source review completed
- [ ] Editor explicitly authorizes the specific redacted release
- [ ] No restricted/private metadata included in publication
- [ ] Corrections channel and audit trail are identified

No autonomous social-media agent or crawler may bypass editorial approval.
