# NEO-PACER FIN-008 / ECC-008 — Asset Recovery & Claims Reconciliation Matrix

## Case
TVMLSM666 v. NEOLPS999

## Purpose
This combined financial/ecclesiastical module reconciles each asserted asset, title, trust interest, bullion lot, account, certificate, ecclesiastical property interest, treasury instrument, and successor claim against one evidentiary structure. It does not assume that an asserted recovery doctrine itself effects a transfer of third-party property. It separates internal ecclesiastical doctrine, claimant-side documentary evidence, external registry/bank/court evidence, competing claims, and present custody.

## Core rule
For every recovery claim, NEO-PACER records:

**source title → alleged defect/loss event → recovery doctrine → claimant → beneficiary → trustee → custodian → competing claimant → operative transfer instrument → value/quantity → current evidentiary status**

A theological, ecclesiastical, indigenous, noocratic, trust, title, or equitable theory is recorded faithfully as asserted. Transfer of a specific asset is classified separately and requires an operative conveyance, decree, judgment, probate order, trust succession instrument, registry entry, bank ledger, custody record, or other legally/evidentially competent bridge.

## Source-derived ecclesiastical governance controls
The Omniversal Church Covenant describes The World Temple as the Omniversal Church for the Global Village and states that it is autonomous in governing its affairs. It authorizes the Church to accept, hold, invest, reinvest, and administer gifts, legacies, bequests, devises, funds and property for its stated religious, charitable and educational purposes. The Covenant separately treats ministerial directors as trustees of ecclesiastical trust property and gives the World Temple Treasurer charge and custody of Church funds and securities, subject to the Church's governance framework.

These provisions establish the internal governance architecture for receiving and administering assets once title or beneficial rights are established. They do not, standing alone, establish title to assets held by unrelated institutions or persons.

## Recovery Classification Codes
- **R0 — ASSERTED:** claim exists in source materials.
- **R1 — INTERNALLY AUTHORIZED:** claimant's own charter/covenant authorizes receipt/administration of the asset class.
- **R2 — EXTERNALLY CORROBORATED:** independent record confirms the instrument, litigation, institution, or event existed.
- **R3 — TITLE BRIDGE IDENTIFIED:** specific transfer/reversion/probate/judgment/registry/bank mechanism has been located.
- **R4 — TITLE BRIDGE AUTHENTICATED:** operative bridge is authenticated by competent primary records.
- **R5 — CUSTODY RECONCILED:** present custodian and ledger/registry position are verified.
- **R6 — BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP RECONCILED:** trustee, beneficiary, encumbrances, and competing claims are resolved.
- **R7 — RECOVERY READY:** evidence package is sufficient for a lawful demand, petition, accounting request, negotiated return, or adjudicative presentation in the competent forum.

No claim may be advanced to R7 merely because an internal covenant declares ownership, sovereignty, secured-party status, or recovery rights.

## Ecclesiastical recovery branch — ECC-008
ECC-008 operationalizes ECC-001 through ECC-007 by requiring one asset-specific record for each claimed recovery from a prior ecclesiastical or temporal holder.

For every claimed Universal Church / Vatican / Crown / state / bank / museum / trust asset, create:
1. **ECC Asset ID**;
2. original historical owner/title source;
3. alleged defect in title;
4. doctrinal or legal basis for reversion/recovery;
5. identity of the claimed successor;
6. operative transfer/adjudication instrument;
7. present registered owner or custodian;
8. restrictions, liens, donor conditions or public-law limitations;
9. valuation;
10. recovery forum and remedy sought.

4. legal owner;

7. custodian;

9. quantity/value/unit;
10. encumbrance/collateral status;
11. last authenticated transaction;
12. successor institution;
13. current confirmation status.

## Claims integrity protections
NEO Title Auditor must flag:
- claimant and custodian being conflated;
- trustee status being treated as beneficial ownership;
- ecclesiastical authority being treated as automatic secular conveyance;
- a later covenant being used as proof of an earlier transfer;
- duplicate bullion lots represented by multiple certificates;
- unregistered title overriding a registered title without an operative judgment or recognized legal basis;
- self-authored financing statements being treated as proof that an unrelated third party consented;
- historical critique of a title source being treated as automatic vesting in the present claimant.
