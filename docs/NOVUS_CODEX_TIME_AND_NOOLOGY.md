## Time architecture transcribed into NEO System

- Nilotic / Natural Time and sundial orientation.
- 19 months × 19 days = 361-day Tagwum year.
- Four internal weeks per month: 5 + 5 + 5 + 4 days.
- Year-1 anchor: June 26, 1945.
- 19 Firsts = 1 Second.
- 19 Seconds = 1 Har.
- 19 Har = 1 Cycle ≈ 380 Gregorian minutes / 6h20m.
- 19 day Har + 19 shadow Har = 38-Har day/shadow circuit ≈ 760 Gregorian minutes / 12h40m.
- A live Har reading requires an explicit circuit-start anchor. NEO does not silently equate Gregorian midnight or an arbitrary timezone boundary with Har 1.
