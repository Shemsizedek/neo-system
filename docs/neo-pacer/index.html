Immutable Record Doctrine

Corrections are additive.

Original public research records remain preserved. Later corrections, findings, authentication updates, or contradictory material should be added to the record rather than silently rewriting prior entries.

Research discipline

Evidence states remain distinct.

Allegations, leads, authenticated artifacts, corroborated facts, internal findings, and external adjudications are not treated as interchangeable states.
