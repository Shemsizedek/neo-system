# Sacred Records of the Moors — NEO Algo Source Layer

## Source

`Let's Set The Record Straight!` identifies itself in its introduction as an excerpt from **The Sacred Records Of The Moor** and states that its purpose is to “set the records straight.” The NEO System stores this material as a source-bound record layer so that its internal hermeneutic, claims, symbols and research method remain available to NEO Algo without being silently rewritten.

## Extracted source themes

### 1. Record restoration

The introduction frames the work around ancestry, sovereignty, rights, reparations, historical continuity and recovery of records that the source says have been omitted, distorted or mislabeled.

Operational consequence: NEO Algo treats historical naming and identity questions as provenance problems. It asks for source lineage, chronology and predecessor records instead of normalizing the latest institutional label.

### 2. Document-producing Factology

The introduction tells the reader to investigate claims and require documents, naming ancient documents, pictures, slabs, tablets, carvings, engravings, records and scriptures as evidence classes.

Operational consequence: **NMX-013 — Produce the Record** instructs the system to seek originating evidence before relying on repetition, prestige or shorthand.

### 3. Ancient Sacred Seal of the Moors

Printed page 113 / PDF page 126 identifies an **Ancient Sacred Seal of the Malians or Moors Mystical Order**, describing an inverted crescent and six-pointed star and asserting later adaptations by several fraternal or Islamic orders.

Operational consequence: symbols are indexed as provenance clues. **NMX-014 — Symbols Require Provenance** prevents visual similarity alone from becoming an automated conclusion about common origin, succession, authority, membership or institutional identity.

### 4. Five P framework

Printed page 114 / PDF page 127 associates five points with:

1. Politics
2. Psychology
3. Philosophy
4. Polytheism
5. Penal System

NEO Algo may use these as a source-derived interpretive framework when analyzing the relevant record tradition.

### 5. Constitutional genealogy claim

Printed pages 115–117 argue that the United States Constitution was patterned on or plagiarized from the Iroquois Great Law of Peace and discuss the Six Nations and matron-led governance.

Operational consequence: the claim is preserved as `SOURCE_HISTORICAL_CLAIM` and routed into Factology / constitutional-genealogy research. Source assertion, documentary parallel, influence evidence and legal consequence remain distinct fields.

## Integrity boundary

The source also contains claims about race, genetics, ancestry and historical populations. Those statements may be preserved as source material when relevant, but NEO Algo must not convert them into automated biological, racial, worth, entitlement or inferiority/superiority classifications of real people.

The reasoning system may investigate provenance, chronology, symbols, historical assertions, institutions and documents. Human dignity is not scored by doctrine.
