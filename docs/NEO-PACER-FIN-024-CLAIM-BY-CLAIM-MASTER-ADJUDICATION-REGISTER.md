# NEO-PACER FIN-024 — Claim-by-Claim Master Adjudication Register

## Purpose
FIN-024 is the master decision register for the accumulated financial, ecclesiastical, succession, treasury, bullion, trust, property, institutional, and governance claims in the NEO-PACER corpus.

The register does not treat every collected document as proven. Each claim is adjudicated separately against the evidence architecture established in FIN-001 through FIN-023.

## Required Claim Record
Each claim entry must include:
- Claim ID
- Claim title
- Claimant(s)
- Respondent/counterparty
- Asset/right/interest claimed
- Amount/quantity if asserted
- Source instrument(s)
- Authentication class A0-A5
- Independence class I0-I5
- Confidence class C0-C5
- Institutional recognition RCO-0 to RCO-5
- Recovery readiness RR-0 to RR-7
- Existing contradictions
- Competing claimants
- Jurisdiction/forum
- Missing evidence
- Next permitted procedural action
- Prohibited premature action
- Current disposition

## Decision Rules
1. Every claim is divisible. A single claim may contain independently supportable and unsupported subclaims.
2. Authentication of a document does not authenticate every statement in it.
3. Internal recognition is not external recognition.
4. Custody is not ownership.
5. Administration is not beneficial ownership.
6. Notice is not acceptance.
7. Repetition is not independent corroboration.
8. A high claimed amount does not increase evidentiary weight.
9. A contradiction must be preserved until genuinely resolved.
10. The absence of current confirmation is not automatically proof of fraud, but it blocks higher recovery-readiness classifications where confirmation is necessary.

## Master Register Rule
No omnibus conclusion is permitted simply because the total corpus is large. Each claim must independently identify:

`source → authentication → authority → title/interest → asset specificity → custody/counterparty → contradiction status → jurisdiction → remedy`

Only after that chain is sufficient may the claim advance under FIN-023.
