# Gate 133 — NEO Institutional Collateral, Credit & Financing Control

Gate 133 governs internal/community credit capacity for Gate 132 institutional accounts.

It records credit facilities, collateral eligibility, haircut schedules, margin agreements, concentration limits, financing terms, utilization and available-credit decisions.

Secured credit fails closed when collateral or margin-agreement evidence is missing. Haircuts reduce collateral value before credit capacity is calculated, and concentration limits can block an otherwise sufficient collateral pool.

A positive credit decision is authorization metadata only. Gate 133 does not disburse funds, originate loans externally, or execute securities financing. Financing terms remain recorded-not-funded until a separate authorized execution path exists.

Credit evidence is digest-chained for later Crown anchoring.
