Research integrity

Provenance stays attached.

Each record carries its source class, research role, source identifier, and current-law verification requirement in an expandable research-details section. That metadata is preserved for serious review without dominating the public browsing experience.

Legal boundary

Research is not adjudication.

Inclusion in the Corpus does not by itself establish current law, jurisdiction, title, liability, or external enforceability. Internal standards are not statutes or court judgments. Verify controlling law and official records with the competent jurisdiction when external legal effect matters.
