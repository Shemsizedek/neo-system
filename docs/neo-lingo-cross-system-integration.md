# NEO Lingo Codex v1.0 — Cross-System Integration

## Purpose
NEO Lingo is the translation layer between canonical NEO terminology and ordinary language. Its job is comprehension, not doctrinal mutation. Domain registries remain authoritative for their own terminology and controls.

## Processing model
`Human request → NEO Lingo intent/translation layer → domain engine → audit/authority checks → response → NEO Lingo presentation layer`

Canonical terms should remain available in the response or metadata whenever translation could otherwise erase a material distinction.

## NEO Algo
NEO Algo uses NEO Lingo at input normalization and output explanation. Plain-language mappings may improve intent classification and decision support, but canonical concepts remain attached to the analytical record. Translation must not be treated as evidence.

## NEO Oracle
NEO Oracle presents accessible interpretations while preserving the distinction among user-defined doctrine, independently verifiable facts, analytical inference, argument, and unknowns. NEO Lingo must never make a metaphysical proposition appear empirically established merely by translating it into scientific-sounding language.

## GISS / NEO LMS
The LMS may display a dual-layer learning model: `Plain Language` followed by `Canonical / Advanced Terminology`. The formal curriculum text remains unchanged unless an authorized curriculum revision is made. Glosses are explanatory aids, not replacement doctrine.

## NEOsync — Digital Etheric Intelligence
NEOsync treats NEO Lingo as a shared adapter available to domain agents. It should translate before routing when translation improves intent recognition, and translate after domain execution when specialized output needs an accessible explanation. Domain validators always outrank the translation layer.

## NEO Law
NEO Lingo may explain internal legal, natural-law, ecclesiastical, contractual, constitutional, statutory, regulatory, common-law, equitable, and procedural terminology in ordinary language. It must preserve jurisdiction and authority boundaries. Internal NEO or Temple terminology does not by itself alter public-law status or create governmental authority.

## Internal NEO Society social norms
NEO Lingo supports understandable norms, consent, dispute prevention, and shared vocabulary. Social translation does not authorize an agent to invent sanctions, titles, obligations, or membership status. Human governance and applicable domain policies remain controlling.

## Pa Sarun Naming System
The existing Pa Sarun registry and NIA-010 validator remain authoritative. NEO Lingo may explain a term such as `Profession of Obligation`, `Major Lesson`, or a validation error in plain language, but it may not translate a candidate name into a different linguistic chamber, relax the language firewall, confer H.R.I., grant Ali/El/Al/Bey/Dey, or bypass human Temple review.

## N.I.A.
Intelligence products may use a two-layer format: an executive plain-language assessment followed by canonical analytical terminology. Provenance, corroboration, confidence, and fact/inference/advocacy separation must survive translation.

## NEO Lifestyle / Creator Media
NEO Lingo can produce audience-adapted explanations for posts, videos, courses, and public education. Public simplification should link back to canonical concepts rather than creating competing definitions.

## Audit rules
1. Preserve the canonical source term.
2. Mark user-defined doctrine or internal terminology as such when context requires it.
3. Do not convert metaphor into empirical fact.
4. Do not let translation override a domain validator or permission gate.
5. Preserve legal and jurisdictional distinctions.
6. Preserve provenance and confidence in intelligence products.
7. Escalate ambiguous mappings rather than silently redefining a term.
8. Version material changes to the Codex.

## Source of truth
Machine-readable mappings live in `registry/neo-lingo-codex.yaml`. Existing domain registries remain authoritative for domain-specific rules.
