# NEO-ACT-003 — Treasury evidence intake

Development-only. The user-provided Community Exchange screenshots show account statements, virtual-dollar ledger activity, a Noone Society proposal, and sovereign guarantee instrument images. These are historical documentary assertions rather than verified cash, liquid collateral, or enforceable claims.

Classify each source separately: statement transactions; instrument issuance; issuer-side obligations; holder-side rights; custody evidence; independent valuation. Do not sum the displayed credits, face amounts, or virtual-dollar balances into verified Treasury reserves without source reconciliation, beneficial ownership proof, independent pricing, and legal/accounting review.

Implemented: conservative evidence classification and time-bounded quote intake validation. No live exchange connector, blockchain indexer, independent signature verification, automatic valuation, dashboard deployment or certified actuarial result.

Next gate: authenticated read-only source adapters, audit hashes, wallet ownership verification, issuer-versus-holder liability maps, multi-asset fixed-point quotes, and authorization-tested dashboard.
