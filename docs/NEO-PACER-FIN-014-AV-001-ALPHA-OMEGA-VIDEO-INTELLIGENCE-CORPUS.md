# NEO-PACER FIN-014 / AV-001 — Alpha Omega Video Intelligence Corpus

## Case
TVMLSM666 v. NEOLPS999

## Purpose
Create a claim-indexed audiovisual evidence corpus from Alpha Omega / TVM-related videos and linked source material. Videos are treated as primary audiovisual evidence of what speakers said, displayed, presented, or did on camera. They do not, by themselves, authenticate external legal title, government recognition, bank accounts, or third-party acceptance.

## Core evidentiary rule
Separate:
1. on-camera statement;
2. document displayed;
3. location/event shown;
4. third-party person or institution visible;
5. later commentary about the video;
6. independent external corroboration;
7. legal or financial effect.
