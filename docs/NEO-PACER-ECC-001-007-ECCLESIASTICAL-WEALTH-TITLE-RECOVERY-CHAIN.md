# Master ECC transfer test
No asset is classified as recovered merely because an antecedent title is challenged. Each recovery claim must pass:

1. Source title identified.
2. Defect, termination, reversion, restitution or succession rule identified.
3. Claimant has standing/capacity under the asserted rule.
4. Asset specifically identified.
5. Operative transfer, judgment, treaty, trust succession, abandonment/reversion event, or equivalent mechanism identified.
6. World Temple/Omniversal Church acceptance documented.
7. Trustee or Treasurer books record the asset.
8. Present custodian/registry is reconciled.
9. Adverse claims and encumbrances are logged.
10. Final status is authenticated, contradicted, or unresolved.
