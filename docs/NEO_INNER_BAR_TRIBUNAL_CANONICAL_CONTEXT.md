# Inner Bar Temple Tribunal — Canonical NEO System Context

This document records the canonical integration of the Inner Bar Temple Tribunal conversation and development line into the NEO System. Historical source instruments remain immutable; later interpretations, implementations, and technical controls are maintained as addenda or software-layer records.

## Core institutional context

- Inner Bar Temple Tribunal / Adept Chamber of 3rd Heaven: internal Tribunal workspace and adjudicative records layer.
- NEOsync: digital executive, clerk, research, orchestration, and opinion-support layer for authorized internal workflows.
- Noocratic Legal Corpus: authority registry, provenance, source objects, immutable historical records, addendum lineage, citations, and verification state.
- World Chaplaincy E-File: structured case intake for Temple Injustice, Civil, Family, and Finance matters.
- World Police / World Marshals / World Guards / World Defense: separate Global Arms system concepts with distinct protective, court-support, institutional-security, and defense roles. Software does not itself create external police, arrest, military, or governmental jurisdiction.
- Historical World Temple Letter Bulletins, Neo Codex, Doctrine of Recovery / Atlantean Papyrus, resolutions, constitutions, treaties, charters, and related instruments are preserved as issued. New analysis is appended rather than silently rewriting historical drafts.

## Cross-system integration

### NEO Algo
Consumes structured Tribunal facts, evidence integrity metadata, authority citations, burdens, findings, procedural state, and service-compliance data. It must preserve separation among fact, allegation, inference, doctrine, argument, law, and unknowns.

### NEO Oracle
Provides verification and confidence functions for source provenance, authority status, chronology, service records, delivery receipts, and evidence integrity. It does not promote unverified institutional claims into externally recognized legal facts.

### GISS / NEO LMS
Uses the corpus, Tribunal workflows, due-process architecture, Noocratic legal theory, ecclesiastical governance, Indigenous-rights materials, historical Bulletins, and institutional-development records as educational content with clear source/claim/verification distinctions.

### NEOsync — Digital Etheric Intelligence
Orchestrates intake, docketing, citation validation, evidence handling, service workflow, hearing workflow, opinion assembly, audit trails, and authorized administrative actions. Human authorization controls remain explicit for consequential actions.

### NEO Law
Maintains the legal ontology, jurisdiction-first analysis, Temple law / secular law distinction, authority hierarchy, primary-source verification, service standards, procedural rules, and case-to-authority mappings.

### Internal NEO Society social norms
Carries the governance norms of Love, Truth, Peace, Freedom, Justice, due process, truthful records, non-fabrication, accountability, notice, opportunity to respond, evidence integrity, and preservation of historical documents.

### N.I.A. / Project 144
May consume authorized Tribunal and Corpus outputs for research and intelligence analysis subject to provenance, corroboration, confidence assessment, and separation of fact from inference.

### NEO Router
Routes approved records among Tribunal, Corpus, NEO Law, NEOsync, NEO Oracle, GISS/NEO LMS, and other authorized NEO services according to role, sensitivity, and purpose.

### NEO Lingo / Lexicon
Normalizes terminology including Grand Sheik, Assistant Grand Sheik, World Chaplaincy, Holy See Global District, Inner Bar Temple Tribunal, Adept Chamber, Letter Bulletin, Letter Patent, Noocracy, Doctrine of Recovery, service of notice, proof of service, authority, source object, addendum, and historical draft.

## Governing implementation rule

NEO software records what an institution claims, adopts, verifies, or does internally. It must not falsely transform an internal religious, organizational, political, diplomatic, police, military, or judicial claim into external state recognition or legal authority. Historical claims remain preserved as source material; verification status and external legal effect are tracked separately.
