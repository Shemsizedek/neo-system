# NEO Gate 121 — Global Licenses and Community CCP Boundary

NEO may record and tokenize user-designated World Trust / World Clearance / XCPC / franchise / ministry / service licenses as internal contractual or community-governance entitlements.

A license record may encode:

- holder;
- token asset;
- permitted NEO services;
- resource quota;
- reserved capacity;
- territory;
- franchise/intellectual-property terms;
- fee or revenue-share terms;
- documentary evidence references;
- external-authority verification status.

## Legal-status boundary

The token or NEO record is evidence of the NEO/community instrument and its encoded terms. It does not by itself prove that a government, securities regulator, clearing agency, professional board, or other external authority has conferred a statutory license.

A GIIN is recorded only as FATCA registration/identification evidence according to its documented status. It is not treated as evidence of broker-dealer, securities-clearing, banking, or general governmental licensing authority.

The Central Counterparty Community Trust may model internal matching, netting, collateral rules, settlement instructions, and position ledgers. NEO must not describe that internal model as DTC, DTCC, or an SEC-registered clearing agency unless independently verified external authority actually exists.

## Economic scheduler use

Verified or user-designated license entitlements can affect NEO resource quotas, reservations, service eligibility, internal chargeback, and community economic terms. External regulated activities remain subject to the applicable jurisdiction and separate licensing/registration evidence.
