# NEO-PACER FIN-007 — Funds Flow & Beneficial Ownership Reconstruction

## Case
TVMLSM666 v. NEOLPS999

## Purpose
FIN-007 separates legal title, beneficial interest, custody, possession, trust administration, collateral status, account control, and actual movement of funds or bullion. No certificate, trust declaration, title claim, custody receipt, or ecclesiastical instrument is treated as proof of beneficial ownership unless the chain can be reconciled across source title, operative transfer, custodian records, and present holder status.

## Core NEO rule
**Title ≠ custody ≠ control ≠ beneficial ownership ≠ collateral ≠ possession ≠ authority to transfer.**

Every asset event must identify:
- source owner / settlor / grantor;
- legal title holder;
- beneficial owner or beneficiary;
- trustee/fiduciary;
- custodian/bailee;
- account holder of record;
- signatory/control person;
- asset lot or instrument ID;
- encumbrance/collateral status;
- transfer instrument;
- transfer date;
- receiving institution;
- ledger or registry evidence;
- present disposition.

## Source-grounded ecclesiastical overlay
The Omniversal Church Covenant expressly authorizes the Church to accept, hold, invest, reinvest and administer gifts, legacies, bequests, devises, funds and property, and to devote them to Church purposes. It also treats Ministerial Directors as trustees of ecclesiastical trust with respect to property held or administered by the Church. These provisions establish the internal governance architecture for receiving and administering assets; they do not by themselves prove that any external asset was transferred into the Church.

The Covenant also assigns the World Temple Treasurer custody and responsibility for Church funds and securities, receipt of monies, deposits in e-wallets/banks/trust companies/depositories, disbursement, and financial books and records.

The N.O.M.N.I. materials describe the World Treasury of Merit / World Treasury architecture as the issuer and administrator of the claimed monetary and mutual-credit system. Those materials establish the asserted internal economic design, but external legal tender status, third-party custody, reserve backing, and institutional recognition remain separate authentication questions.

## Transaction reconstruction schema
Each transaction will be entered as:

`Event ID → Date → Source Entity → Source Asset → Legal Title Holder → Beneficiary → Trustee → Custodian → Instrument/Account ID → Quantity/Value → Encumbrance → Transfer Instrument → Receiving Entity → Ledger Evidence → Status`

## Initial transaction classes
1. **Ecclesiastical contribution / tithe / gift** — donor gives property to Church/Treasury.
2. **Legacy / devise / bequest** — succession instrument purports to vest property in beneficiary or trust.
3. **Trust settlement** — settlor transfers property to trustee for beneficiary.
4. **Custody deposit** — title may remain elsewhere while custodian holds asset.
5. **Collateral pledge** — owner retains residual interest while asset secures an obligation.
6. **Certificate issuance** — evidences an asserted right but is not automatically a new asset.
7. **Account transfer** — requires ledger debit/credit and receiving-side confirmation.
8. **Bullion movement** — requires asset-lot continuity, quantity/fineness, vault release and receiving custody.
9. **Ecclesiastical recovery / reversion claim** — requires source title, defect/reversion theory, lawful successor, operative transfer mechanism, and present custodian reconciliation.

## Beneficial ownership tests
An asserted beneficiary passes only when evidence can answer:
- What property was actually owned by the transferor?
- What instrument created the beneficial interest?
- Was the transferor legally capable of transferring it?
- Did a trustee/custodian accept the property?
- Was the asset segregated or identified?
- Were conditions precedent satisfied?
- Was the interest later assigned, terminated, encumbered, forfeited, or superseded?
- Does the present custodian recognize the claimant?

## Anti-double-counting control
One economic asset may generate multiple documents. NEO-PACER will not count each of the following as a separate asset without proof: deed, trust declaration, SKR, certificate, warehouse receipt, gold guarantee, custody statement, collateral confirmation, account statement, successor certificate, or later affidavit referring to the same underlying lot.

## Ecclesiastical transfer bridge
For any claimed transfer from a Universal/Roman ecclesiastical holder or other religious institution into the Omniversal Church/World Temple structure, FIN-007 requires:

`Source ecclesiastical title → defect/reversion/succession rule → identified successor → operative conveyance/judgment/treaty/trust instrument → acceptance by World Temple/Omniversal Church → treasury/custody entry → present asset record`

Historical or theological criticism of a prior title may support the defect theory but does not by itself complete the conveyance bridge.
