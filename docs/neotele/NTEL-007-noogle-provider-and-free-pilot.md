# NTEL-007 — Noogle Telecommunications provider designation

NeoTele's primary organizational service provider is **Noogle Telecommunications**, using the **Noogle Fi** routing fabric and NEO Wire membership systems. This is a **brand/operator role** assigned internally, not evidence of FCC/USAC authorization, a wholesale cellular network agreement, or ability to mint eSIM profiles.

## Free-service model
Chaplaincy-sponsored connectivity is the first proposed plan, with a target subscriber monthly price of $0. Plan availability remains **false** until a funded service arrangement, coverage, carrier eligibility, emergency calling capability and consumer disclosures are verified. Never call the plan “free Lifeline” unless furnished by a participating ETC with verified National Verifier/NLAD workflow and appropriate authorization.

## Required before activation
1. Verify Noogle Telecommunications legal entity and applicable FCC, state and USAC obligations.
2. For Lifeline directly: obtain ETC designation and, as applicable, approved compliance plan, 498 ID and USAC Lifeline Study Area Code (SAC). Otherwise partner contractually with an authorized ETC and identify its responsibilities.
3. Contract an actual cellular service provider or authorized MVNO platform with eSIM provisioning, phone numbers, coverage and emergency calling.
4. Connect real provider credentials and certified remote SIM provisioning systems; do not generate fake eSIM QR codes.
5. Activate the Noogle partner adapter in staging, security-review signed provider evidence, then separately approve production deployment.
