# Orange Chip, World Credits and Central Counterparty Trust — Capital Formation Gate
**NEO-GENIUS-004 | DRAFT — Not an offer to sell securities, token issue, registered clearing operation or operating liquidity pool.**

## Mission
Support lawful formation of businesses and project special-purpose vehicles, clear investor rights, Bitcoin/Counterparty-linked records and interoperable reporting. Keep NOMNI/World Credits, non-security rewards, credit contracts and actual issuer equity separate.

## Issuer authorization is more than a Bitcoin asset issuance
Every purported tokenized equity asset requires:
- Valid legal entity and organizational documents; authorized shares or membership interests.
- Issuer resolution and actual signed instrument defining dividends, votes, redemption, transferability and dilution.
- Correct transfer agent/registrar or legally authorized books and records, shareholder mapping and cap-table reconciliations.
- Applicable registration/exemption and securities counsel; state law and offering disclosure.
- Investor KYC/sanctions and relevant AML responsibilities; offering intermediary/placement limitations; marketing approvals.
- Reconciliation of on-chain transfers with legal transfer limitations and ownership entitlements.
- No false liquidity, pricing, guaranteed-return or state-endorsement claims.

**Current repo:** `src/explorer/orangeChipVerification.ts` explicitly states listing status alone does not verify off-chain legal equity, credit, royalty, fund or other rights. `modules/orange-esop` similarly requires legal issuer plans before restricted ESOP units are transferable. Preserve these rules.

## Possible funding channels (choose legal route before soliciting)
- **Regulation Crowdfunding:** up to $5 million in a rolling 12 months for eligible issuers, solely via SEC/FINRA registered intermediary, required disclosures; securities generally have one-year resale limits with exceptions.
- **Regulation D 506(b):** restricted private placement; general solicitation generally prohibited.
- **Regulation D 506(c):** permits general solicitation to accredited investors only with reasonable verification; other requirements apply.
- **Regulation A:** subject to qualification and other requirements; evaluate for future larger raises.
- **Commercial debt or non-security contractual sales:** evaluate individually; naming debt as a World Credit does not determine its legal classification or exempt it from financial-services rules.

**Counterparty/Counterwallet/NEO DEX:** a payment or record technology choice does not by itself authorize distribution, brokerage, securities trading or secondary liquidity. For exchange/ATS functions obtain qualified regulated partner or legal exemption. OTC/penny stock and antifraud rules may also apply.

**September 2026 SEC conditional innovation exemption:** targeted certain permissioned venues and liquidity providers for tokenized National Market System stocks; it does not establish an unrestricted exemption for newly formed companies, private microcaps, pooled investor proceeds or Orange Chip listings.

## Central Counterparty Trust functional boundary
- **Currently proposed:** contractual escrow policy, milestone payment coordination, multisignature signoff via qualified providers, segregation, dispute resolution, risk dashboards and audit evidence.
- **Do not claim:** registered securities clearing agency, principal-to-principal securities novation, deposit insurance, investor guarantee, securities transfer agent licensing, government approved custody or risk-free settlement.
- Escrowed assets, client funds and the trust's own capital require legal title / beneficial ownership clarity, segregation, reconciliations and regulatory review. Smart contracts can reduce some operational risks but do not remove credit, custody, liquidity or legal enforcement risk.

## Pilot issuer order
1. One verified small-business operating issuer, with actual entity name, authorized capitalization, signed cap table and owner authority.
2. One verified asset/project SPV, only after site control/valuation and project-specific sources and uses.
3. Tokenized issuance concept on TESTNET or private sandbox with no solicitations or on-chain securities sales.
4. If counsel selects a legal offering exemption, document eligible intermediary or qualified investors, marketing and post-offering compliance.
5. Secondary liquidity only after legal market/intermediary approvals and documented transfer restrictions.

## Asset-specific terminology
- **NOMNI** is described in NEO source as having a *market-observed value, not a guaranteed redemption peg*; independently verify historical agreement, rights, issuance and on-chain control before describing it as stable or reserve-backed.
- **World Credits** require a contract-level legal analysis for monetary claims, issuer obligations, lending, payments, stablecoins, securities or nonredeemable community credits.
- **Orange Chips** must preserve investor ownership and voting distinctions; a token without signed legal entitlement is only a record, not an automatic share.
- The name **Central Counterparty Trust** does not itself grant central-counterparty regulatory authority.
