NEO Guardian Terms of Use

Effective August 28, 2026.

NEO Guardian is a defensive security utility. It helps users inspect device posture, review applications, open trusted Android security controls, and perform opt-in network checks.

No guarantee of absolute security

No mobile application can guarantee prevention or detection of every compromise. Guardian findings are advisory signals and should be combined with Android updates, strong authentication, trusted software sources, and professional incident response when warranted.

User-controlled actions

Guardian does not secretly modify system settings. Actions that affect Android security configuration are presented visibly and normally open Android's own settings interfaces.

Third-party services

Optional live features may open or contact third-party services such as ipify, GitHub Pages, Android security documentation, and VirusTotal. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Responsible use

Guardian is intended for devices and accounts you own or are authorized to administer. Do not use it to monitor, interfere with, or access another person's device without authorization.
