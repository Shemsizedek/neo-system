# Gate 135 — NEO Institutional Servicing, Collections & Credit Performance Fabric

Gate 135 manages funded advances after Gate 134 settlement.

Advance -> scheduled payment -> verified payment evidence -> fee/interest/principal allocation -> performance history -> delinquency/cure/restructuring/default/recovery records -> closure.

Payments are not posted without verified evidence. Delinquency is based on explicit due dates and elapsed days, not subjective labels. Default records require both a policy reference and evidence reference.

The service records covenants, restructuring terms and recovery accounting, but does not conduct coercive collections or external enforcement.

Performance output is prepared for later WCA / NEO Metro 2 and Proof-of-Payment adapters. Gate 135 does not claim external consumer-credit-reporting authority; Metro 2 mapping remains adapter-required until credentialing and reporting paths are independently verified.
