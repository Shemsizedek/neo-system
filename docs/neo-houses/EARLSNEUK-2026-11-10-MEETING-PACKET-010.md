## Commercial underwriting routes

**A. Buy completed homes:** obtain agent/developer authorized prices and title, appraisal, comparable sales, insurance, taxes, HOA, occupancy and gross-to-net monthly carry. Seller claim of "low $300Ks" is **not** a verified price per unit. Compare one-household buyer underwriting and affordable-income fit.

**B. Conditional lot option + build:** obtain actual lot acquisition basis, time-to-close, utilities/sitework, drainage, vertical contract pricing, hard + soft costs, financing and interest carry, unit-level contingency, permit schedule and investor/homebuyer rights. Never count proposed credits/tokens or speculative donor proceeds as project cash.

**C. Authorized owner/developer JV:** legal entity and capital commitments, promoter/sponsor roles, carried interest/sweat equity documentation, construction contract, financing, return waterfall, project accounting, conflicts and exit mechanics. Requires signed party authority and qualified counsel.

## Build-to-own protections
- Beneficiary purchase rights or equity credits require enforceable written contracts; they do not transfer title automatically.
- Labor, training credits, wage obligations and public affordability must remain separate. No public program may require religious participation to obtain housing.
- Funding may come from verified sponsor cash, qualified debt, approved grants, documented seller financing or properly structured third-party equity. NOMNI or Orange Chips are not automatically spendable U.S. dollars or guaranteed repayment sources.
- Require fair housing, consumer finance, independent title, construction surety, insurer and local building/zoning review.
