## Two separate NEO financing vehicles
**A. Qualified affordable rental multifamily SPV** — for an open NOFA, only if property eligibility and developer experience can be established and the application is complete by the posted deadline. Avoid assuming reserve funds, token liquidity or unawarded grants. Obtain verified property package (title/option, survey, appraisals, rent roll, T12, pro forma, rehab scope, construction bids, permitted use, floodplain/environmental, debt availability, sponsor statements, compliance plan).

**B. Build-to-own new construction SPV** — start with verified land/site control and a true costs/sources underwriting model. Investigate legally available funding and private co-investment, and scale to city program thresholds when appropriate. Keep household ownership/purchase contracts separate from investors' SPV participation; do not equate apprenticeship level with deed vesting.

## Sources and uses — financial close controls
**Uses:** land/property acquisition; title, surveys, inspections; horizontal and vertical construction; licensed subcontractors; environmental and resilience; permits; insurance; architecture; utilities; labor wages; financing and lender fees; required reserves; contingency; sales/lease-up and exit costs.

**Permitted sources (count only after written evidence):**
- Cash paid-in sponsor equity.
- Documented approved senior debt / construction draws.
- Signed and funded partner equity / legally offered securities proceeds.
- Seller notes under executed documents.
- Executed grants or government loans, subject to matching, affordability and compliance conditions.
- Philanthropic contributions only if restricted-use requirements and cash availability are verified.

**Not countable as spendable capital without verification:** NOMNI face value, untraded World Credit or Orange Chip quotes, future expected donations, verbal public-agency conversations, anticipated token-pool proceeds, an announced grant for which NEO has not been awarded money.

The companion **NEO-GENIUS-004-Capital-Readiness-Model.xlsx** (distributed separately) provides editable input cells; project costs, funding gap, gross sale margin, rental NOI, DSCR, Houston size screen and a source register. Defaults intentionally reflect **unverified capital**, not profitability.

## Build-to-own safeguards
- Housing eligibility, underwriting, affordability covenants, deed conveyance and purchase terms must be documented independently of any Temple rank or apprenticeship.
- Existing Civitas proposal splits nominal NOMNI wages into optional housing-credit targets but expressly states `legalWageCompliance: not-reviewed` and `deedTransferAuthorized: false`. Never treat a proposed split as authority to withhold mandatory wages, deduct wages unlawfully, substitute tokens for wages, or guarantee property ownership.
- Account for paid labor, volunteer work, proposed housing credits and contractor invoices under applicable wage/hour rules, benefits, prevailing-wage requirements where applicable, workers' compensation, consumer lending and mortgage rules.
- Publicly assisted housing must meet fair housing, equal-access and religious-nondiscrimination requirements; public benefits cannot depend on religious participation. Keep privately funded Temple mentorship separate from publicly assisted beneficiary selection.
- Deal underwriting must include downside scenarios for cost overruns, build delays, financing changes, flood/insurance costs, occupancy and affordable-sale constraints.

## Required gating decisions
- [ ] Specific land/property and verified legal control.
- [ ] Qualified developer/contractor and project team.
- [ ] Signed/dated bids, closing cost schedule and contingency.
- [ ] Realistic cash sources and reserve accounting.
- [ ] Statutory housing eligibility, labor/fair housing protections and legal ownership documents.
- [ ] Sources == uses at closing, with independent reconciliations.
- [ ] Counsel and lender review of any token-related project securities or consumer-credit product.
- [ ] No grant application represented as submitted or approved until confirmed.
