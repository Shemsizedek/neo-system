# Pa Sarun Naming System — NEO Integration Specification

## Authority and scope
This module records the World Temple of Karast, Branch Temple No. 24 naming framework as an internal NEO System governance and audit domain. NEOsync may validate, explain, render, and prepare naming records, but final conferral of titles, names, houses, naturalization status, and Temple authority remains a human Temple decision.

## Ceremony context
The Naturalization Procedure occurs within the Holy Shahaddah Ceremony during the Holy Day Gathering for Moorish Science Temple members. The procedure and the ceremony are related but are not interchangeable terms.

## Rendering rule
The public/formal royal name is concise. Never concatenate Haru, Nebti, Neter, location/ruler, degree, diplomatic, and Pa Hanument fields into an expanded public full name.

The applicable rendering formula is determined by the Major Lesson. Council authorization is separately checked before H.R.I. may be rendered. General-member forms of address are Brother, Sister, Kin, or Kinfolk plus the formal name.

## Degree architecture
The counted 144-degree curriculum is organized as four Major Lessons: 1-36 Masonic-themed, 37-72 Magi-themed, 73-108 Shriner-themed, and 109-144 Mystic-themed. Major Lessons 5-9 are honorary for the Elect and Elite and do not increase the counted total beyond 144. Each counted degree requires a Profession of Obligation.

For the 144° naming layer, authorized title forms include Neb, Nebu, Noonebu, and Neb Nebu; adopted royal-family forms include Nun, Nupu, and NoopooH.

## Naturalization suffix registry
Ali = Syriac; El = Ashuric; Al = Arabic; Bey = Turkish; Dey = Ottoman. These are granted once during the applicable naturalization procedure and are not repeatedly re-earned at later degrees.

## NEOsync audit behavior
A naming request should produce a validation result before a rendered name. At minimum NEOsync checks the requested Major Lesson, degree, profession of obligation where applicable, title authority, language family, naturalization suffix, Royal House mapping, and lesson-specific naming formula. Ambiguities or conflicts are escalated for human Temple review rather than silently repaired.

The machine-readable source of truth is `registry/pa-sarun.yaml`.
