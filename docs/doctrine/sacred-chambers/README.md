## Sacred Chambers

### Wife Chamber — Solar Order
The senior adult family chamber for covenantal governance, household administration, mentorship, estate stewardship where separately authorized, and preservation of family doctrine. The Wife Chamber provides governance for the adult Concubine Chamber subject to consent, written policy, safeguarding rules, and applicable law.

### Concubine Chamber — Lunar Order
A private adult covenantal chamber associated with companionship, service or employment where separately contracted, education, and possible progression toward Wife status. Membership does not itself create marriage, employment, compensation, inheritance, or property rights. Any employment relationship must be documented separately and comply with applicable labor law.

### Mother / Holy Mother Chamber — Earth Order
The Holy Mother function is the senior safeguarding, lineage, education, welfare, and continuity office within the Sacred Chambers. It provides doctrinal oversight and protects chamber members from coercion, retaliation, exploitation, and conflicts of interest. No chamber authority supersedes an adult member's consent or civil rights.

### Daughter Chamber — Stellar Order
A lineage, education, and succession category only. It is not an intimate or sexual chamber. Minors are categorically excluded from adult intimate, concubinage, employment-for-intimacy, or sexual rites and agreements.

## Consent and safeguarding baseline

All adult intimate relationships and rites are voluntary and revocable. No sexual act is an employment duty, pledge obligation, initiation requirement, debt payment, disciplinary sanction, or condition for housing, compensation, education, advancement, inheritance, or access to essential support. Refusal or withdrawal of consent cannot create a penalty.

Confidentiality obligations protect legitimate private information but do not prohibit reporting suspected crimes, abuse, harassment, discrimination, unsafe conditions, regulatory violations, or speaking with counsel, medical professionals, law enforcement, regulators, or other legally protected persons.

Discipline may govern internal membership and ceremonial standing, but it cannot confiscate vested property, earned wages, statutory rights, or personal identity credentials without an independent lawful basis.
