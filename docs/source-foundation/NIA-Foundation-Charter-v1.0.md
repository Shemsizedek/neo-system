# N.I.A. Foundation Charter v1.0

## Establishment
The **Novenary Intelligence Agency (N.I.A.)** is the private intelligence, research, strategy, knowledge, security-planning, and operational-support framework serving the NEOsync / Project 144 ecosystem.

## Mission
Transform information into organized knowledge, knowledge into strategy, and strategy into measurable action while preserving institutional continuity.

## Departments
1. Office of the Director of Intelligence — executive coordination and priorities.
2. NEOsync Intelligence Systems — AI agents, automation, knowledge systems, and orchestration.
3. Research & Intelligence Bureau — historical, institutional, market, and open-source research.
4. Global Relations Division — international research, partnerships, liaison, and cultural relations.
5. Economic Intelligence Division — business intelligence, financial analysis, market research, and forecasting.
6. Media & Communications Division — public messaging, publishing, brand strategy, and digital media.
7. Archives & Records Division — records preservation, Source Foundation maintenance, and institutional memory.
8. Education & Knowledge Development — curriculum, training, publications, and knowledge transfer.

## Revenue Model
Permitted business lines may include research and intelligence consulting, AI systems and workflow services, publishing and media, education and professional development, business intelligence, and other approved services consistent with applicable requirements.

## Staffing Structure
Executive leadership coordinates department directors, professional staff, analysts, researchers, developers, strategists, communications personnel, and registered AI agents.

## Operational Protocol
N.I.A. uses a repeatable cycle: **Collect → Analyze → Plan → Produce → Deploy → Review**.

## Approval Model
AI agents are advisory by default. Material external actions, representations, financial commitments, publication, account changes, and other consequential execution require the approval level defined for the relevant mission or system.
