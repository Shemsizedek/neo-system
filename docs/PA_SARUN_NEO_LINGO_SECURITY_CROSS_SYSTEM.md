## Naming controls

The controlling principles include:

- H.R.I. requires authorized Temple Council status.
- Naturalization suffixes are earned once and remain fixed through later degree progression.
- Major Lesson language firewalls are enforced; Magism is the defined exception for authorized blending.
- Neter naming is restricted to Major Lesson 8.
- Sarun is system terminology, not an ordinary personal-name candidate.
- Hotep is not used alone as a complete personal name.
- Nisut-Bit names render the location or territory rather than the literal words `Nisut-Bit`.
- Expanded ritual categories are not stacked into one public formal name.

## NEO Lingo role
