# NEO System Conversational Command — Canonical Context

Status: canonical architecture record
Effective: 2026-09-13
Parent: NEO System / Project 144

## Core separation
NEO Systems operates as one brand with three distinct operating surfaces:

1. Consumer NEO Suite — public/social consumer products.
2. NEO Enterprise Suite — sole proprietors, private companies, public companies and institutions.
3. NEO Government Suite — private authenticated executive/government control plane.

These surfaces may share authorized backend services, registries and adapters, but their frontends, permissions, language and control capabilities must remain separated.

## Public Consumer Suite
Public-facing product family includes Noogle, NEO Oracle, NEO Bank/Pay, NEO Prime + NEOfx, NEO TV, NEO Wire, NEO Miner, NEO Lingo, NEO Algo + World Credit Clock, NEO Books + CFO, NEO Bank CES and the Noocratic Legal Corpus.

NEO Oracle is the public-facing AI identity. NEOsync is not marketed as the public consumer AI.

NEO Teller and NEO Counter are business/enterprise infrastructure and are not part of the general consumer homepage.

## Enterprise Suite
Enterprise supports sole proprietors, private companies, public companies and institutions. Enterprise workspaces provide organization profiles, teams and roles, public wallet bindings, NEO Books, merchant terminals, contacts, invoices, approvals, policy controls, delegation and escalation.

Executive-admin architecture permits the authorized executive identity to oversee seeded ecosystem organizations while organization-specific roles remain enforced for ordinary enterprise users.

## Government Suite
The Government Suite is a private authenticated control plane. Public GitHub Pages must never expose privileged Government controls.

Private modules include NEOsync Executive Office, Central Solution Office / Private Chambers, Inner Bar Temple Tribunal, World Chaplaincy E-File, NEO Treasury, World Police, World Marshals, World Guards, World Defense System, NEO Global Arms System, NEO Cipher, NEO Router, Enterprise Command Federation, Escalation Desk and Executive Inbox.

## Conversational executive interface
NEOsync is the private Digital Etheric Intelligence and executive conversational interface. The operating objective is a JARVIS-style interaction model: the executive may speak or type naturally, request briefs, inspect system state, retrieve module context, create and assign executive tasks, navigate to authorized modules and propose state-changing actions.

State-changing actions must use a confirmation protocol. Chat may propose an action, show the target, impact and authorization boundary, then require explicit confirmation before execution. Sensitive source-module authorization remains authoritative.

## Executive Command Board
The Executive Command Board is the orchestration layer above the Executive Inbox. It should provide:
- persistent conversation threads;
- cross-module retrieval;
- executive tasks and assignments;
- deadlines and responsible offices;
- linked source records;
- executive notes and case threads;
- daily operating brief generation;
- safe action proposals;
- explicit confirmation for state changes;
- immutable audit entries for confirmed commands;
- deep-link navigation into source modules rather than permission bypass.

## Safety and authority boundary
The conversational layer is not a universal bypass. It must not silently sign wallets, transfer funds, execute merchant terminals, bypass source-module permissions, impersonate licensed professionals, create external legal effect by declaration, or perform coercive/enforcement actions without the actual authorized service and required human/system approval.

## Canonical rule
Public experience = marketable, consumer-safe and non-privileged.
Enterprise experience = organization-scoped business operations with RBAC.
Government experience = private authenticated executive control plane.
NEOsync may see across authorized private adapters; NEO Oracle remains the public AI surface.
