# NEO Algo — Etheric, Alchemical & Doctrine Architecture

## Purpose

This phase extends NEO Algo from provenance-first reasoning into a structured noological intelligence layer for mental patterns, ways of thinking, social norms, meditations, affirmations, insights, understandings, reasons, rationales, morals, ethics, principles and NEO Philosophy.

The implementation preserves source framing while separating three classes:

1. **SOURCE_DERIVED** — a teaching or pattern stated in supplied source material.
2. **NEO_SYNTHESIS** — an explicit NEO System interpretation derived from multiple sources or current NEO doctrine.
3. **OPERATIONAL_CONTROL** — a software governance rule used to keep actions coherent, evidence-aware, restorative and non-erasing.

## Source pattern: Breaking The Spell

The supplied text repeatedly presents a chain of:

`Right Knowledge → Right Wisdom → Right Overstanding → Right Thinking → Right Words / Right Works → Right Action`

It also argues that knowledge must be organized in sequence and that institutional conditioning can produce non-questioning mental habits. NEO Algo operationalizes those concepts as provenance recovery, chronology construction, assumption audits and action traceability.

The source also contrasts 6-Ether and 9-Ether. In software, these are treated as **symbolic doctrinal poles**, never as scientific measurements or permanent biological classifications of people.

- **6-Ether symbolic pole**: contraction, fragmentation, deception, coercion, exploitation, disorder.
- **9-Ether symbolic pole**: creative order, coherence, truthfulness, stewardship, reciprocity, reason, knowledge/wisdom/overstanding.

## Dynamic polarity: 6 / 9 and Yin / Yang

NEO Algo models polarity dynamically rather than as a static good-person/bad-person identity.

- `SIX_ETHER` and `NINE_ETHER` are system-state labels.
- `YIN`, `YANG`, and `DYNAMIC_BALANCE` describe the current polarity phase.
- A numeric potency score from `-9` to `+9` is an **internal symbolic control value**.

The score is calculated from normalized signals such as truthfulness, coherence, stewardship, reciprocity, creativity, nature alignment, provenance integrity, exploitation, deception, coercion and fragmentation.

This score is used to route review and reflection. It does not determine empirical truth, legal status, biological identity, scientific frequency or moral worth.

## Alchemical action pipeline

Every consequential action can be reviewed through seven transformation stages:

1. **Calcination** — remove ego, inherited prestige, artificial urgency and unnecessary assumptions.
2. **Dissolution** — loosen rigid categories and reintroduce nature, embodiment and relationship.
3. **Separation** — distinguish fact, interpretation, doctrine, inference, legal effect, valuation and desired outcome.
4. **Conjunction** — recombine only what remains coherent across provenance, reciprocity, ethics and evidence.
5. **Fermentation** — generate restorative and creative alternatives.
6. **Distillation** — simplify to the clearest truthful purpose with the least unnecessary harm.
7. **Coagulation** — embody the refined action with accountability and stewardship controls.

The supplied *Luciferian Conspiracy* source associates Tehuti/Thoth with writing, science, medicine and alchemy. NEO Algo uses alchemy as a **transformation/governance metaphor**, not as a substitute for technical or scientific validation.

## NEO System action rule

A NEO action should not be considered fully reviewed until the system can answer:

- What is directly observed?
- What is interpretation or doctrine?
- What is the provenance?
- What chronology is relevant?
- What community self-interpretation is relevant?
- What are the consequences for people, nature and future generations?
- What opposing pole or missing counterforce has not yet been considered?
- Does the remedy reproduce the same erasure, coercion or exploitation it opposes?
- What survives the alchemical transformation sequence?
- What is the smallest truthful action that improves coherence and stewardship?

## Safety and integrity boundary

NEO Algo must preserve the supplied texts without silently editing them, but operational software must not convert racial, ethnic, religious or biological generalizations in historical/doctrinal sources into automated classifications of real people.

The source layer may record that a text makes such claims. The action layer instead evaluates behaviors and system states: deception, coercion, fragmentation, extraction, truthfulness, reciprocity, stewardship and evidence integrity.

This prevents the software from reproducing the very pattern of dehumanization or category erasure that NEO Maxims are intended to detect.
