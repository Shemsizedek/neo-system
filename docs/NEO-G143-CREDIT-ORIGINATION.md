# Gate 143 — NEO Credit Origination & Contract Execution Fabric

Gate 143 manages the controlled transition from a Gate 142 offer to Gate 134 funding readiness.

Offer -> acceptance -> jurisdiction-specific disclosure package -> acknowledgment -> versioned agreement -> external/multisignature signature -> conditions precedent -> funding readiness.

An accepted or signed agreement is not a funded transaction. Funding readiness only means the configured prerequisites are satisfied; Gate 134 still controls authorization and settlement.

Private keys remain outside the service. Jurisdiction-specific disclosure templates and e-signature enforceability require separately verified policy and legal requirements.
