## Operational funnel
Visitor -> clear offer and case studies -> contact/qualification form (company, desired capability, deployment constraints, target budget and timeline, permission to contact) -> NEO Deal Desk quote (non-binding) -> approved statement of work and authorized signature -> verified invoice/payment -> delivery scheduling -> CRM/ledger reconciliation.

## Payment safeguards
- Do **not** enable live NEO Counter crypto fulfillment based on URL return parameters or client-reported 'success'. Require signed server-side processor event or independent chain observation with confirmation policy, reconciled to an invoice and payment intent.
- Do not create fake testimonials, phantom earnings, countdown urgency, or investment-return promises.
- No public investment solicitation or tokenized security sale through this funnel without jurisdictional legal/compliance authorization.
- Never expose customer PII, wallet secrets, raw payment credentials in GitHub.
- Preserve premium pricing policy and legal/commercial approval gate.

## Definition of done
- Public service landing page approved, mobile-tested, and accessible.
- Lead form submits to controlled destination; spam controls + privacy disclosure tested.
- Unique lead reference tracked through CRM, proposal and contract.
- At least one real processor/bank checkout rail proven in sandbox and production, with webhook/event deduplication and evidence of settlement.
- Operator-visible reconciliation dashboard: qualified leads, proposals, signed deals, invoices, **settled cash** and recurring net revenue separately.
- Before launch: publication/terms approval, service capacity, tax/payment recipient verification and refunds/cancellation policy.

## 7-day execution queue
D1 verify public landing page, account contact and checkout capabilities; D2 draft offer pages; D3 lead-form security tests; D4 connect operator-controlled leads inbox/CRM; D5 draft first tailored proposals under existing pricing policy; D6 verify chosen payment rail and invoice trail; D7 release only after evidence-backed acceptance.

## KPI definitions
Qualified lead = consented real inquiry meeting project requirements.
Pipeline = non-binding commercial opportunities.
Bookings = signed contract value, not cash.
Cash collected = reconciled cleared payment less refunds and chargebacks.
MRR = recurring contracted revenue that is active and billable; report cash receipts separately.
