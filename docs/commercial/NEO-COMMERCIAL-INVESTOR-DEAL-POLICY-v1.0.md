**Owner:** Private Chamber of Shemsizedek / NEO System

## Purpose
Establish one commercial doctrine for all NEO agents, bots and automation handling retail sales, institutional licensing, white-label development, custom systems architecture, strategic partnerships, investor relations and acquisition inquiries.

## Core commercial rule
NEO is priced as an AI operating environment, enterprise application platform and systems-architecture practice—not as commodity website development or hourly freelance labor.

`registry/neo-commercial-pricing.yaml`.

## Selling doctrine
1. Sell outcomes, infrastructure and institutional capability.
2. Prefer license + implementation + recurring support over source-code sale.
3. Do not negotiate against ourselves.
4. Never discount autonomously.
5. Quote from approved bands and increase price for complexity, integrations, security, compliance, scale, exclusivity or accelerated delivery.
6. Treat IP transfer, exclusivity, perpetual rights and custom ownership as exceptional premium transactions.
7. Preserve a written scope, assumptions and approval record for every material quote.

## Investor doctrine
Investor communications must distinguish:
- replacement cost;
- internal strategic asking price;
- financing valuation;
- actual third-party valuation;
- revenue/ARR;
- projections;
- historical results.

Agents may automate investor discovery, research, qualification, CRM routing, factual materials and draft outreach.

Agents may not autonomously:
- make binding securities offers;
- promise returns;
- guarantee appreciation;
- select or misstate an exemption;
- accept funds;
- issue final equity/token/revenue-share terms;
- execute agreements;
- represent legal or regulatory approval without verified authority.

Live investor solicitation and final terms require an approved compliance lane and human authorization.

## Autopilot deal flow
`Discover -> Enrich -> Classify -> Qualify -> Price -> Draft -> Compliance Gate -> Human Approval (when required) -> Send/Present -> Follow-up -> Negotiate within mandate -> Escalate exceptions -> Close by authorized human/process -> Archive`

Commercial SaaS and service outreach may proceed under configured business-development permissions. Securities-related outreach must use the stricter investor gate.

## No-low-ball rule
Any proposed price below a canonical minimum is automatically an exception and must be routed to the human decision owner with:
- requested discount;
- reason;
- strategic value;
- term length;
- expected lifetime value;
- concessions requested;
- recommended counteroffer.

Silence or lack of response is never approval.

## Versioning
Pricing may be revised as product maturity, revenue, deployments, market conditions, security posture, IP portfolio and customer traction change. All changes should update both this policy and the machine-readable registry.
