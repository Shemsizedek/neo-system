## Four meanings of "one NOMNI equals one unit"

1. **CES mutual-credit parity:** a denomination rule or credit accounting statement, with an explicitly named unit and participant-approved rules. It need not equal a market dollar.
2. **CES Virtual-Dollar parity:** an internal book-unit/rate in CES, with historical statement records, separate from cash held by a regulated bank.
3. **NOMNI index parity:** a benchmark definition or valuation methodology, requiring reference basket, market evidence, weights and publication schedule; benchmark points are not guaranteed execution prices.
4. **USD cash redemption:** current issuer obligation to deliver $1 USD per NOMNI through a lawful redeeming institution, on published terms, fully supported by legally eligible reserve assets, independently attested and regulated where applicable.

All four may coexist **only when separately defined and reconciled**. The first three do not automatically prove the fourth. Do not falsely deny or erase the historical design while preventing representations of unverified cash backing.
