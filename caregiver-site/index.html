Our mission

Make community care easier to navigate.

Caregiver Connect is being developed as an accessible starting point for family caregivers, older adults, and people facing barriers to digital services. The initiative connects practical resource navigation, learning, and community collaboration without claiming to replace professional care.

How Neotherapy fits

Understanding, reflection and resilience.

Neotherapy, drawing on Noology's emphasis on understanding and sound reasoning, is proposed here as a non-clinical, voluntary reflective learning pathway alongside caregiver navigation and digital education.

Participants could explore personal stressors, communication practices, reflective habits, and sources of support. Any future programs would require defined facilitator standards, safeguarding practices, accessibility review, and evaluation.

Scope: Neotherapy here is not presented as evidence-proven medical treatment, diagnosis, psychotherapy or replacement for licensed healthcare. No therapeutic efficacy is claimed. Regulated services and sensitive health data would require appropriate professional oversight, consent and legal safeguards.

Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations

Can I enroll for services here?

Not yet. This page is a public concept and resource directory; no live registration or caregiver service is connected.

Is Neotherapy a clinical treatment?

No. It is described here solely as proposed voluntary education and reflection, not a substitute for medical or mental-health services.

Is QDA an official partner?

No formal partnership is represented here. Joint work is contingent on mutual review, approval and written agreement.

Will NEO Pass be required?

No account is needed to read this page. An optional caregiver dashboard would require further privacy and security review.
