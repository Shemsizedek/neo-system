# NES-006-COM — Microgrid Engineering and Commissioning Gates

**No physical field control is authorized by this document.**

## Gate 0 — Site / jurisdiction
Identify utility service territory, authority having jurisdiction, applicable adopted electrical code, utility interconnection standards, emergency plans and licensed engineer of record.

## Gate 1 — Studies
Site survey; one-line diagram; measured load profiles; load flow; short circuit; equipment ratings; conductor ampacity; voltage drop; grounding/bonding; protection coordination; reverse power / DER behavior; islanding feasibility. Document all assumptions and sign-offs.

## Gate 2 — Protection and cyber/OT
Qualified relays/inverters/transfer equipment; anti-islanding and synchronization functions; cybersecurity segmentation; unique device identities; least privilege; command allowlist; safe fail states; offline local protective operation.

## Gate 3 — Pre-energization
As-built SLD; conductor and insulation tests by qualified personnel; breaker and relay setting verification; controlled inspection; emergency shutdown; utility permission and permits.

## Gate 4 — Controlled commissioning
Documented switching plan under authorized field personnel; confirm protective trip and transfer function; record baseline metering; controlled loss-of-grid simulation only where utility/authority permits; rollback plan; incident log.

## Gate 5 — Operation
Operator sign-off; verified alarms; measurement provenance; maintenance schedule; backup recovery exercises; audit and change control.

The NEO Oracle/cloud supervisor remains advisory-only until these gates are signed off. This specification must not be used as a switching procedure.
