# NES-012 — Microgrid Operational Readiness Decision Standard

Status: engineering checklist only. No electrical operating permission granted.

Evidence gates with accountable signed approvals:
1. SITE: service territory, jurisdiction, utility agreement, permits, engineer of record.
2. DESIGN: verified load flow and short circuit studies, one-line, grounding, coordination, equipment ratings.
3. CYBER_OT: segmentation, identity, command policy, local protection independent of cloud/AI.
4. HARDWARE: listing/certification and factory/field tests, protection, battery fire safety.
5. METERING: approved revenue meter accuracy, calibration, clock sync, signed provenance.
6. COMMISSION: witnessed pre-energization, switching/rollback plan, protection tests and utility permission.
7. OPERATIONS: accountable operators, incident response, maintenance, emergency procedures and audits.

Any missing evidence blocks FIELD_CONTROL readiness. Simulated scenarios and advisory planning can proceed subject to appropriate access controls. The NEO Oracle cannot clear any gate, and field operators must hold independently validated credentials and authority. Gate artifacts must include evidence IDs, reviewer identity, decision timestamp and the equipment/site revision being approved.
