# NEO Miner v0.15 — Commercial Production Release & Productization

This layer converts validated NEO Miner hardware and Digital NEO Miner contract capacity into sellable, supportable products.

## Product Families

### Physical Hardware
- NEO Miner One — entry commercial SHA-256 appliance
- NEO Miner Pro — higher-density professional appliance
- NEO Miner Rack — rack-oriented fleet deployment
- NEO Immersion Rack — immersion-ready fleet deployment

### Digital Products
- Digital NEO Miner Spark — 25 TH/s contract-backed allocation
- Digital NEO Miner Core — 100 TH/s contract-backed allocation
- Digital NEO Miner Titan — 500 TH/s contract-backed allocation
- Digital NEO Miner Sovereign — 1 PH/s contract-backed allocation

Digital products must remain backed by verified physical capacity and must not be represented as independently generating Bitcoin without corresponding mining infrastructure.

## Commercial Release Package

The release package must include:
- production SKU table
- costed BOM and landed-cost structure
- MSRP / channel / wholesale pricing fields
- gross-margin targets
- warranty and service terms
- installation and safety manuals
- customer provisioning workflow
- device activation workflow
- fulfillment and shipment records
- support SLA definitions
- distributor/reseller terms
- return/RMA integration
- release checklist

## Pricing Principles

Every physical SKU should expose:
- materials cost
- assembly cost
- test cost
- packaging cost
- logistics allowance
- warranty reserve
- channel margin allowance
- operating overhead allocation
- target gross margin

Every Digital NEO Miner contract should expose:
- contracted TH/s or PH/s
- contract duration
- power/hosting assumptions
- pool fee
- maintenance/service fee
- payment currency
- FX quote source
- expected versus actual delivered hashrate
- estimated versus actual BTC attribution
- settlement rules

No contract may guarantee BTC production or investment return.

## World Currency Payments

The checkout architecture may support fiat, BTC, XCP, NOMNI, and other World Currency assets only when a real payment rail, exchange rate source, liquidity path, jurisdictional approval, and settlement process exist.

A listed currency is not automatically an accepted currency.

## Warranty Framework

Warranty terms are configuration-controlled by SKU and region. Suggested commercial structure for planning purposes:
- hardware defects: 12-month limited warranty target
- fans/consumable wear parts: shorter configurable coverage
- customer misuse, unsupported overclocking, unauthorized modification, liquid damage outside approved immersion configurations, and improper electrical installation may be excluded subject to final legal terms

Warranty policy must be reviewed before commercial launch.

## Support Tiers

- Standard: ticket/email support, firmware/security updates
- Professional: priority support, fleet diagnostics, advanced replacement options
- Enterprise: named account support, deployment assistance, response-time SLA, integration support

## Activation Flow

Factory release → serial/identity registration → fulfillment → customer receipt → local provisioning → account association → secure device enrollment → pool/wallet configuration → acceptance test → mining activation → fleet monitoring.

## Digital Contract Activation

Customer order → currency/payment validation → KYC/compliance gate if required → payment confirmation → contract acceptance → capacity reservation → physical hashpower allocation → share attribution → settlement ledger.

## Commercial Launch Gate

Commercial release is blocked until all required items are satisfied:
- PVT production-release gate passed
- target-market regulatory obligations resolved
- production BOM frozen
- suppliers approved
- factory work instructions approved
- EOL fixtures validated
- firmware release signed and reproducible
- security provisioning operational
- warranty/RMA process live
- customer documentation approved
- product liability and commercial terms reviewed
- payment rails actually integrated for each advertised payment method
- Digital NEO Miner capacity-backing controls verified

## Status

v0.15 establishes the productization and commercial-release control system. It does not itself certify, authorize, or place any hardware or financial product on the market.
