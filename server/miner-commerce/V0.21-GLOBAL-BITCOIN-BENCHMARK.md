# NEO Generator v0.21 — Global Settlement Router + Standard Benchmark Agreement for Bitcoin

## Purpose
Create a voluntary global trade and settlement framework centered on Bitcoin, Counterparty XCP, and NOMNI, while preserving clear distinctions among reserve asset, protocol asset, application asset, mutual credit, fiat currency, payment execution, custody, and benchmark publication.

## Historical reference
Bretton Woods (1944) is used as a design precedent for international monetary coordination, consultation, exchange-rate discipline, and trade facilitation. It is not represented as having predicted or mandated Bitcoin.

## U.S. policy reference
The March 6, 2025 White House Executive Order established a United States Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a separate U.S. Digital Asset Stockpile. NEO's Standard Benchmark Agreement is independent, private/non-governmental infrastructure and must never imply White House, Treasury, IMF, World Bank, or other governmental sponsorship or recognition without documentary authorization.

## Proposed trade-bloc stack
1. BTC — reserve/settlement benchmark and proof-of-work monetary root.
2. XCP — Counterparty protocol asset for Bitcoin-native tokenized assets and market operations.
3. NOMNI — NEO ecosystem asset that may participate only where a verifiable market, legal basis, liquidity path, and enabled settlement rail exist.
4. NEO Wire — settlement-routing and reconciliation layer.
5. CES — mutual-credit trade ledger connected through exchange-specific coordinator gateways.
6. World Currency — registry and multi-currency quotation layer.
7. NEO Miner / Generator — Bitcoin production, verified hashrate allocation, and mining-contract settlement infrastructure.

## Standard Benchmark Agreement for Bitcoin (SBA-BTC)
The agreement is a proposed market standard, not a treaty and not a claim of sovereign authority.

### Principles
- voluntary participation;
- transparent methodology and published versions;
- open auditability of benchmark inputs;
- independent source timestamps;
- no invented or stale price observations;
- explicit distinction between reference price and executable liquidity;
- no guaranteed convertibility of XCP or NOMNI;
- jurisdiction-aware compliance, sanctions, tax, and consumer-protection controls;
- custody and settlement remain separate from benchmark publication;
- dispute and correction procedures for erroneous observations;
- cryptographic hashing of each published agreement version.

## Benchmark model
Initial research configuration:
- BTC weight: 70%
- XCP weight: 15%
- NOMNI weight: 15%

These are governance-controlled research weights, not a fixed economic claim. Production publication must have valid, contemporaneous market observations for every included component, or the benchmark is `UNAVAILABLE`.

## Unified settlement router
The router may evaluate:
- NEO Wire bank/ACH/wire;
- BTC on-chain;
- Lightning;
- Counterparty/XCP and enabled assets;
- CES mutual credit;
- additional approved World Currency providers.

Candidate rails are scored using compliance status, customer preference, currency support, jurisdiction, liquidity, fee, and settlement speed. A failed primary rail may fail over only to another compliant, enabled rail.

## Global-trade controls
A global trade-bloc transaction is not activated solely because an asset is listed. It requires:
1. enabled payment/settlement rail;
2. authoritative quote;
3. jurisdiction/compliance approval;
4. verified settlement or posted CES trade;
5. valid order/contract reference;
6. sufficient inventory or backed hashrate;
7. immutable audit event.

## Non-claims
NEO System must not state that:
- Bretton Woods prophesied Bitcoin;
- SBA-BTC is a government treaty;
- the White House has endorsed NEO, XCP, NOMNI, CES, NEO Wire, or SBA-BTC;
- NOMNI or XCP has guaranteed liquidity or redemption;
- a benchmark observation is an executable market price unless an actual execution venue confirms it.
