NEO Nous OS access

Choose whether Nous OS may read permitted information tied to your NEOpass account. This is optional. You may withdraw permission at any time.

Before you decide

  • Read only. Nous OS can list approved agents, verify source information and request read-only analysis through authorized adapters.
  • No financial authority. This does not authorize transfers, wallet signing, contracts, purchases, publishing or deployments.
  • Limited duration. Consent expires after 24 hours. Nous access tokens expire within 120 seconds and require an active account at issuance.
  • Withdrawal. You can revoke consent here. This blocks new Nous tokens, but tokens already issued can remain valid until expiration or signing-key revocation.
  • Account records. NEOpass stores consent status, expiration and revocation cutoff. Nous OS stores separate planning receipts for authorized operations.

A recent NEOpass sign-in is required to change access. If you recently signed in, you may need to sign in again to confirm your choice.
