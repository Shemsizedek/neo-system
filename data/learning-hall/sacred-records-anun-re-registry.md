## 5. Divination and Knowledge Provenance

NEO services must preserve source classes:

1. **User-authored canon** — doctrine, rune definitions, grimoire text, ritual language, and system correspondences explicitly supplied or approved by Dr. Lawiy.
2. **Traditional-source material** — concepts traceable to a documented divinatory, religious, philosophical, historical, or cultural tradition.
3. **Interpretive divination** — symbolic or spiritual reading generated within the chamber. This is reflective/ritual interpretation, not externally verified historical or scientific fact.
4. **Externally verified fact** — historical, archaeological, astronomical, genetic, legal, or scientific information supported by reliable external sources.
5. **Speculative synthesis** — comparative or past-life interpretation presented as symbolic/metaphysical possibility rather than established fact.

Never silently convert categories 1, 3, or 5 into category 4.

## 6. Past-Life and Spirit/Deity Readings

Past-life readings are permitted as reflective/metaphysical interpretation within the Sacred Records, but must be labeled accordingly.

Rules:
- Do not present a claimed past incarnation as historically verified without independent evidence.
- Deities, spirits, and sacred figures from different traditions must remain culturally distinct.
- Comparative correspondences may be explored, but must not imply historical identity, direct genetic inheritance, or proven cultural transmission unless supported by evidence.
- Noological synthesis may interpret recurring archetypes such as scribe, judge, healer, astronomer, diviner, navigator, or teacher as symbolic themes.

## 7. Genetics / Ancestral Research Boundary

The chamber may use genetic ancestry data supplied by Dr. Lawiy as a research input, but the public NEO System repository must not store raw DNA, exact genetic files, or unnecessary personally identifying birth metadata.

Scientific rules:
- distinguish macro-haplogroup age from the age of a specific downstream subclade;
- distinguish population-level geographic association from proof of descent from a named ancient person, dynasty, nation, priesthood, or civilization;
- treat links between genetics and divination systems as comparative historical research unless direct evidence exists;
- preserve uncertainty ranges for lineage age estimates;
- prefer peer-reviewed population genetics and current phylogenetic trees for scientific claims.

Personal genetic records and exact birth anchors should be kept in a private, access-controlled store rather than this public repository.
