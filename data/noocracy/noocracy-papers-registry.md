## 1. Purpose

This registry promotes the Noocracy Papers working sessions into a canonical NEO System source for NEO Algo, NEO Oracle, GISS / GISD LMS, NEOsync (Digital Etheric Intelligence), NEO Law, internal NEO Society social norms, and other doctrine-aware services.

The Noocracy Papers are to be treated as a philosophical and constitutional counterpoint to the Federalist Papers. The Federalist Papers defended the American federal constitutional project; the Noocracy Papers develop the case for Noocracy, the Global Village, a World Parliament, Noocratic republicanism, and governance grounded in Nous, sound reasoning, ethical judgment, knowledge, liberty, peace, justice, public trust, and collective security.

## 2. Permanent Drafting Canon

Every Noocracy Paper must be addressed exactly:

> To the People of the Global Village:

Unless expressly directed otherwise, each paper is written in the first person of H.I.M. Dr. Lawiy Zodok Shamu-El.

When adapting a Federalist Paper, the NEO System must not merely summarize it. Every material argument, objection, analogy, historical illustration, political principle, and conclusion in the corresponding Federalist Paper must be preserved in argumentative function and revised through the Noocratic perspective.

Required method:
1. Identify the original Federalist proposition or concern.
2. Preserve its argumentative function.
3. Translate or answer it through Noocratic principles.
4. Reframe national-only assumptions toward the Global Village where appropriate.
5. Distinguish local autonomy from destructive political fragmentation.
6. Preserve liberty, minority rights, prosperity, peace, justice, and public trust as governing objectives.
7. Prefer wisdom, evidence, ethical deliberation, transparency, and sound reasoning over faction, domination, mob passion, or personal ambition.

## 3. Core Noocratic Governance Principles

- Noocracy means governance through Nous: wisdom, sound reasoning, cultivated knowledge, ethical judgment, and disciplined public deliberation.
- The Global Village is humanity understood as an interconnected political community while preserving legitimate local identities, nations, districts, customs, and jurisdictions.
- A World Parliament is the deliberative institutional expression of the Global Village.
- Noocratic republicanism opposes both tyranny and uncontrolled factionalism.
- Unity does not require cultural erasure or the destruction of lawful local administration.
- Governance should harmonize competing interests through reason rather than faction, coercion, corruption, or numerical domination alone.
- Representation may refine public judgment, but representation must itself be subject to wisdom, ethics, accountability, and protection against capture.
- The causes of faction cannot be entirely abolished without destroying liberty; the proper objective is to control destructive effects while cultivating higher civic reason.
- Political scale may be an asset when a larger sphere protects diversity, diffuses concentrated factions, improves collective security, and preserves local competence.
- Noocratic institutions must distinguish doctrine, historical interpretation, verified external fact, and project aspiration.

## 4. General Introduction Framework

The Noocracy Papers collectively examine:
- The utility of the VILLAGE to political prosperity.
- The insufficiency of the present unionized democratic federation to preserve the VILLAGE.
- The necessity of a government sufficiently energetic to accomplish this object.
- The conformity of the proposed constitution to the true principles of Noocratic republicanism.
- Its analogy to local and state constitutional arrangements.
- The additional security its adoption affords to liberty, prosperity, peace, justice, and preservation of government.

## 6. Source and Provenance Rules

The Noocracy Papers may draw from:
- the Federalist Papers as structural and argumentative templates;
- earlier Noocratic Papers / houseofcrowns drafts where available;
- Noological doctrine and Neoteric Method materials;
- relevant NEO System constitutional, educational, economic, and social-norm sources;
- external historical sources when research or verification is requested.

NEO services must preserve source classes:
1. **Source-derived** — directly supported by supplied/project materials.
2. **Noocratic interpretation** — philosophical reinterpretation or synthesis authored within the NEO project.
3. **Externally verified fact** — supported independently by reliable historical, legal, scientific, or other external sources.
4. **Project aspiration / institutional claim** — a declared NEO objective, jurisdictional assertion, governance model, or organizational position that is not automatically an externally established fact.

Never silently convert category 2 or 4 into category 3.
