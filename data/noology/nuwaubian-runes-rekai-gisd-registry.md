## 4. GISD / Global Independent School System Curriculum Mapping
GISD uses a DIY/adopt-and-adapt curriculum architecture tied to the 144 Temple Degrees. Every learner begins at Degree 1 regardless of age; rigor and assignment complexity scale by developmental level.

Education bands:
- Degrees 1–5: elementary/kindergarten band
- Degree 6 onward: middle-school band
- Degree 9 onward: high-school band
- Degree 13 onward: university band
- Degrees 13–21: pathway for learners adding to doctrine
- Nu Omni Academy and Amen Institute: honorary/vocational/trade pathways open from Degree 9
- Full Temple system: 144 degrees

Portfolio evidence may include essays, reports, excerpts, research papers, blogs, vlogs, demonstrations, objects, projects, goals and obligations. Learners 13 and younger may complete schoolwork at home, school/temple buildings or shared learning environments. Older learners may receive mobile/location-based assignments where appropriate and safe. Initiation includes bringing a stone/crystal as a symbolic object.

The GISD model is designed for adoption and local adaptation by participating temple districts, schools and jurisdictions while maintaining a common Noological core.
