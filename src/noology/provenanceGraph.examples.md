# Provenance relation examples

- `ORIGINATED_BY`: an artifact, doctrine, project or instrument and its documented originator.
- `ASSIGNED_TO`: a documented assignment from one holder to another.
- `TRANSFERRED_TO`: a conveyance, custody or asset-transfer event.
- `SUCCESSOR_OF`: a dated succession relationship between entities or offices.
- `FILED_BY`: a filing and the filing entity.
- `ISSUED_BY`: an instrument or asset and its issuer.
- `DERIVED_FROM`: an explicitly documented derivative or source relationship.
- `CORROBORATES` / `CONTRADICTS`: evidence relationships; neither substitutes for source-level analysis.

These relations are evidentiary graph semantics. They do not independently create legal rights or adjudicate disputed claims.
