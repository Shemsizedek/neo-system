NEOfx + NEO DEX

Two distinct tickers share one market-data contract: NEOfx is the reference pricing and foreign-exchange surface; NEO DEX represents executable Counterparty market activity. Prime can consume both without confusing indicative pricing with executable liquidity.

NEOFXBTC, XCP, NOMNI and world-currency reference pricing.
NEODEXCounterparty orderbook/trade ticker for markets with actual executable liquidity.
Prime RoutingBoth feeds should route through the authenticated NEO backend/Router; GitHub Pages never holds private provider credentials.
Until the backend endpoint returns verified quotes, the tickers intentionally show “feed offline” or “no data” instead of fabricated prices. This is the production-safe behavior.
