NOOGLE FINANCE • MARKETS

Live market rails for the NEO economy.

NEOfx provides reference foreign-exchange and cross-asset pricing. NEO DEX displays verified Counterparty market activity. The two are intentionally separate so Noogle Finance can show valuation context without presenting a reference conversion as an executable trade.

Designed for reuse across NEO Prime, NEOpay, NEOscan, NEO Exchange, NEO Statements and other approved NEO market surfaces.
