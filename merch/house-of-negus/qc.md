# Artwork QC
- Transparent background for print artwork unless the design intentionally requires a field.
- PNG/SVG source retained; no accidental white/black rectangular background.
- Pixel artwork: target <= 4000 x 4000 and platform-compatible resolution.
- Vector: closed curves; paths converted; avoid lines under 1 mm for plotter printing.
- Confirm spelling, crop/safe area, contrast, and ownership/licensing.
- Spreadshirt printability review remains authoritative after Partner Area upload.
