NEO Knowledge / KNOWINDEX

Knowledge Index & Knowledge Economic Index

KNOWINDEX is the sponsor-described Bitcoin/Counterparty knowledge-asset initiative associated with NEO Knowledge. The Foundation links public knowledge resources, consented contributions and source-backed observations to its proposed evidence model. A live issuance state, token rights or blockchain update is not verified by this page.

NK-01–03

Resources, sources & permissions

Distinct eligible resources, attribution evidence and authority to publish or access each item.

NK-04–06

Access, preservation & participation

Availability checks, byte-hash integrity and documented consented participation.

NK-07–08

Learning & knowledge economy

Verified learning results, licensing, engagements and actual receipts. Token market value is not counted as economic activity.

Read KNOWINDEX data contractWorld LibraryKNOWINDEX methodology

The NEO Crown Chain can consume permission-reviewed evidence metadata after explicit authorization. All eight pilot measures currently have unreported values, and no Bitcoin commitment, signing or Crown Chain production writes are claimed.
