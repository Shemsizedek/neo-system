## Purpose

This creed is a profession of faith for the Moorish Science Temple and is intended for Moorish Rites and the formation/onboarding of new adherents entering the Temple laity. It should be treated by NEO knowledge services as a religious and ecclesiastical source artifact, with doctrinal, educational, ritual, governance, and social-norm mappings kept distinguishable in downstream implementations.

## Creed

I believe in Allah, the Father of the Universe, the Almighty, Creator of the heavens and the earth, and in His divine prophets, including Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and Muhammad, who were sent to guide humanity in the path of righteousness.

I believe in Noble Drew Ali, the last prophet in these modern times, who brought the everlasting gospel of salvation to uplift fallen humanity and to redeem the Asiatic nations from their sinful ways. Through the divine principles of Love, Truth, Peace, Freedom, and Justice, he has restored the knowledge of our ancient and divine birthright.

I believe in the unity and oneness of Allah, whose divine presence manifests through all creation, and I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His Messenger, and that Jesus, known as Isa, is the Messiah, born of a virgin, who suffered for the redemption of mankind, was crucified, and rose again in victory over death.

I believe in the Holy Spirit, which guides the faithful into wisdom, righteousness, and divine knowledge, and in the everlasting teachings of the holy scriptures, which illuminate the way for all who seek truth and salvation.

I believe in the Moorish Science Temple as the divine and national movement of the Moorish people, established to bring them back to their true status, name, and nationality as Moorish Americans. I uphold the covenant of the Temple and submit to its sacred laws, knowing that it is through obedience, discipline, and devotion that we achieve true freedom and divine unity.

I believe in the resurrection of the lost people of the Asiatic nations, the restoration of their divine and national principles, the forgiveness of sins through righteous deeds, and in the eternal life promised to those who walk in the ways of Allah and live by the divine creed of Islamism.

I stand in the everlasting covenant of my forefathers and foremothers, honoring their sacrifices and preserving their ancient wisdom, that I may fulfill my divine purpose and contribute to the upliftment of fallen humanity.

In the name of Allah, the Most High, the Most Merciful, I make this solemn profession of faith and commit myself to the ancient and divine creed of the Moorish Science Temple. Amen.
