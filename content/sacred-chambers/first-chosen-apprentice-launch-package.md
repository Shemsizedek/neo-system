# Sacred Chambers of Nun — First Chosen Apprentice Launch Package

Status: Operational launch package
Module: NEO-SACRED-CHAMBERS-001
Audience: Adults only

## Purpose
This package operationalizes the first Chosen Apprentice cycle: invitation, orientation, covenant, 30/60/90-day formation, creator assignments, competency tracking, economics records, confidentiality, and succession.

## Invitation
The Chosen Apprentice is invited into a structured creator-development and Noological formation process. The invitation is not a promise of marriage, intimacy, housing, employment, income, rank, or financial support. Acceptance creates a mentorship and development relationship only.

Provide the candidate:
- Sacred Chambers Master Architecture
- Chosen Apprentice Protocol
- Consent & Power Firewall
- 90-Day Formation Plan
- Creator expectations
- Privacy/confidentiality standards
- Competency ledger
- Succession rules

## Entry Conversation
Before appointment, the candidate should be able to explain:
- what Sacred Chambers of Nun is;
- what Noological Erotic Culture means within the project;
- the difference between institutional participation and private intimacy;
- the right to decline or withdraw;
- the difference between historical terminology and modern NEO coinages;
- the role of study, creator work, service, and stewardship.

No intimate disclosure is required for admission.

## Apprentice Covenant
This covenant is ceremonial and ethical. It is not a substitute for contracts, releases, employment agreements, housing agreements, financial documents, or legal consent.

### Apprentice Commitments
- study before representing;
- verify before claiming;
- create before imitating;
- protect privacy;
- respect consent and boundaries;
- distinguish history, symbolism, doctrine, and modern invention;
- contribute meaningful creator work;
- preserve participant dignity;
- avoid coercion, manipulation, and exploitative dependency;
- serve the community;
- document work for future apprentices.

### Temple / Program Commitments
- maintain voluntary participation;
- separate intimacy from rank and advancement;
- avoid retaliation for refusal, withdrawal, or changed boundaries;
- state financial and creator arrangements clearly;
- respect privacy and agency;
- provide defined learning objectives;
- evaluate competence rather than sexual participation;
- preserve historical accuracy;
- maintain a process for concerns and escalation.

## Ceremonial Statement
"I enter the Sacred Chambers as a student, creator, and steward of knowledge. I retain my mind, body, conscience, and boundaries as my own. I accept responsibility for what I learn, what I create, and what I teach. I will not confuse authority with ownership, secrecy with silence, or symbolism with fact. I will learn deeply, create intentionally, serve meaningfully, and prepare the way for those who come after me."

## 90-Day Formation Plan

### Days 1–30 — Threshold
Focus: orientation and intellectual grounding.

Required:
- master architecture study;
- protocol study;
- Noology orientation;
- Sacred Chambers canon orientation;
- consent and power-boundary training;
- Ninsat historical-accuracy rule;
- Divine Feminine Economy overview;
- visual-language study;
- observe one content-development cycle;
- private learning journal;
- one research assignment;
- three creator concepts.

Deliverables:
- mission reflection;
- terminology accuracy exercise;
- three content concepts;
- one service contribution;
- first competency review.

### Days 31–60 — Creation
Focus: convert study into original production.

Required:
- choose one primary creator track;
- assist with one published piece;
- create one original concept from brief to draft;
- participate in production review;
- learn publishing workflow;
- complete one Noology / relationship-culture assignment;
- complete one Divine Feminine Economy research assignment;
- complete one service task.

Deliverables:
- first credited creator contribution;
- documented production workflow;
- one research note;
- one service record;
- second competency review.

### Days 61–90 — Stewardship
Focus: reliability, judgment, and knowledge transmission.

Required:
- lead or co-lead one small creator project;
- document the workflow for reuse;
- participate in canon / terminology review;
- assist with privacy, archive, or production administration;
- complete strengths, weaknesses, and boundary reflection;
- prepare 90-day portfolio;
- propose next-stage development plan.

Deliverables:
- portfolio;
- documented workflow;
- stewardship project;
- self-assessment;
- mentor assessment;
- advancement recommendation.

## Advancement
Advancement requires:
- completed orientation;
- demonstrated understanding of the consent firewall;
- competent doctrine and ethics;
- at least one creator contribution;
- satisfactory reliability and service record.

Higher advancement follows the same rule: demonstrated capability and ethical conduct.

## Confidentiality
Confidentiality protects unreleased work, private participant information, IP, and safety. It does not prevent a person from seeking legal, medical, therapeutic, financial, emergency, or other support, and it must never be used to conceal coercion, threats, fraud, abuse, or unlawful conduct.

## Creator Economics Record
For monetized work, record:
- project;
- contribution;
- ownership / license terms;
- compensation method;
- revenue share if any;
- approval status;
- publication channel;
- date;
- administrator.

Creator compensation and intimate relationships remain separate.

## Succession Record
A Lineage Mentor nominating a candidate should document:
- candidate name or internal identifier;
- basis for nomination;
- demonstrated competencies;
- creator / service contributions;
- ethics / boundary history within the project;
- areas needing development;
- candidate's independent acceptance or decline;
- review outcome.

Nomination creates no right to admission, intimacy, money, housing, relationship status, marriage, or rank.

## First Apprentice Completion Gate
The first apprentice becomes eligible for succession review only after completing:
Orientation → Noology Formation → Canon Study → First Creator Project → First Publication → Service Contribution → Stewardship Assignment → Reflective Review.

Only then may the apprentice nominate a possible successor for independent review.
