# Shelton Estate & Co. — NEO Estates Architectural Design Catalog

Status: Canonical design-development catalog
Module: NEO-HOUSES-001
Version: 1.0

## 1. Development Identity
NEO Estates is the Shelton Estate & Co. built-environment standard for NEO Houses, creator residences, educational properties, hospitality, commercial acquisitions, and selected business/warehouse conversions.

Core formula:
**Clean air + controlled sound + intentional light + natural texture + contemplative space + productive technology + dignified beauty.**

The catalog is a planning standard, not a substitute for licensed architectural, engineering, building-code, accessibility, fire-safety, electrical, mechanical, zoning, or medical advice.

## 2. Spatial Zoning Standard

Every NEO Estate should separate the property into functional zones.

### Zone A — Arrival / Threshold
Purpose:
- transition from public environment to NEO environment;
- identity, calm, orientation, security and hospitality.

Typical elements:
- covered entry;
- vestibule;
- symbolic threshold art;
- seating;
- controlled lighting;
- plantings;
- shoe/storage area where appropriate;
- discreet security and access control.

### Zone B — Living / Fellowship
Purpose:
- communal living;
- meals;
- conversation;
- family and cohort gathering;
- informal study.

Typical elements:
- shared living room;
- dining area;
- kitchen;
- lounge;
- courtyard connection;
- flexible gathering furniture.

### Zone C — Learning / Creator
Purpose:
- study;
- research;
- podcasting;
- streaming;
- writing;
- design;
- editing;
- entrepreneurship.

Typical elements:
- study library;
- production studio;
- podcast booth;
- editing bay;
- flexible classroom;
- meeting room;
- equipment storage;
- high-speed wired network.

### Zone D — Neotherapy / Reflection
Purpose:
- guided reflection;
- contemplative practice;
- relaxation;
- wellness programming;
- small-group education.

Typical elements:
- Neotherapy chamber;
- quiet room;
- breathwork / meditation room;
- soft seating;
- dimmable lighting;
- acoustic treatment;
- optional sound system;
- washable natural-feeling surfaces.

### Zone E — Private Residential
Purpose:
- bedrooms;
- bathrooms;
- personal retreat;
- resident privacy.

Standards:
- acoustic privacy;
- lockable storage;
- controlled temperature;
- appropriate daylight;
- direct escape paths;
- no mining infrastructure.

### Zone F — Service / Utility
Purpose:
- laundry;
- mechanical equipment;
- electrical;
- storage;
- janitorial;
- network rack;
- building controls.

### Zone G — Mining / Productive Compute
Purpose:
- ASIC mining or other heavy compute only where underwriting supports it.

Requirements:
- physically separated from bedrooms, learning, healing and creator zones;
- dedicated electrical design;
- heat extraction;
- acoustic isolation;
- fire review;
- filtered air path as required;
- service access;
- monitoring;
- no shared return-air path with occupied wellness spaces unless engineered appropriately.

### Zone H — Landscape / Healing Garden
Purpose:
- outdoor reflection;
- edible landscape;
- walking;
- informal teaching;
- social decompression.

## 3. Room-Type Catalog

### NEO Great Room
A flexible living/fellowship room combining hospitality, discussion and presentation.

Target qualities:
- warm neutral palette;
- adaptable seating;
- dimmable layered light;
- concealed AV;
- direct garden connection when possible.

### NEO Study Library
Functions:
- reading;
- research;
- tutoring;
- small-group study.

Features:
- perimeter shelving;
- task lighting;
- quiet HVAC;
- charging and hardwired data;
- writable wall or presentation surface.

### Creator Studio
Functions:
- video;
- photography;
- social content;
- livestreaming.

Features:
- controllable lighting;
- acoustic absorption;
- flexible backgrounds;
- power and data;
- equipment lockup.

### Podcast / Voice Booth
Features:
- isolated wall and door assembly;
- quiet ventilation;
- acoustic panels;
- network and balanced power;
- simple visual identity wall.

### Neotherapy Chamber
Recommended program:
- 120–250 sq ft for small residential deployments;
- 250–500 sq ft for larger houses / group work;
- no fixed medical equipment by default.

Features:
- high acoustic privacy;
- dim-to-warm lighting;
- indirect perimeter light;
- adjustable floor and seated-use configuration;
- natural textures;
- optional low-level audio system;
- blackout capability;
- independent temperature control where feasible;
- secure storage;
- easy-clean surfaces.

Use:
- reflective education;
- relaxation;
- guided practice;
- Noology / Neotherapy instruction;
- Reiki-inspired wellness practice.

No claims that the room diagnoses, cures or treats disease unless separately supported and appropriately licensed.

### Reflection Cell
A smaller one-person or two-person quiet room.

Target:
- 45–90 sq ft;
- no screens by default;
- dimmable warm light;
- acoustic privacy.

### Fellowship Kitchen
Purpose:
- household economics;
- nutrition education;
- communal meals;
- hospitality.

Features:
- durable surfaces;
- large pantry;
- efficient appliances;
- shared meal prep;
- accessible storage.

### Apprentice / Creator Bedroom
Target:
- compact private room;
- work surface;
- acoustic door;
- storage;
- comfortable task light;
- high-speed data.

### Steward Suite
Optional room/suite for property steward or resident operator.

### Enterprise Room
A small office / incubator space for business formation, finance, product development and meetings.

## 4. Material Palette

### Core
- warm white plaster / mineral-tone walls;
- natural or engineered wood;
- stone or stone-look durable flooring;
- matte black / obsidian accents;
- bronze, brass or copper details;
- linen, wool-look, cotton and other tactile textiles;
- low-gloss finishes.

### Accent Language
- black;
- ivory;
- warm sand;
- deep earth;
- muted green;
- Nile-inspired blue-green;
- gold / brass;
- copper.

Avoid:
- clinical fluorescent white;
- cheap glossy finishes;
- excessive visual clutter;
- novelty Egyptian décor;
- overuse of symbols without architectural purpose.

## 5. Lighting Scene Standard

### Dawn
- gradual warm-to-neutral illumination;
- low output;
- circulation path emphasis.

### Study
- neutral task lighting;
- high visual clarity;
- low glare.

### Creator
- high-output controllable lighting;
- RGB/production lighting confined to creator spaces where needed.

### Fellowship
- warm layered light;
- table and wall emphasis;
- reduced overhead intensity.

### Temple / Reflection
- low, warm indirect light;
- perimeter glow;
- optional candle-like points of light;
- no harsh direct fixtures.

### Night
- low-level amber path lighting;
- minimal blue-rich light;
- safe circulation.

## 6. Acoustic Standard

Priority hierarchy:
1. bedrooms;
2. Neotherapy / reflection;
3. study;
4. podcast / creator;
5. fellowship;
6. mechanical;
7. mining / compute.

Design tactics:
- resilient channels where appropriate;
- acoustic insulation;
- solid-core doors;
- seals;
- isolated equipment pads;
- duct attenuation;
- vibration control;
- strategic separation by closets, corridors or service zones.

## 7. Landscape Standard

Minimum preferred elements where site allows:
- shade trees;
- native / climate-suitable planting;
- herbs;
- edible landscape;
- seated contemplation zone;
- walking loop or path;
- water element only where maintainable and safe;
- night lighting kept low and shielded;
- rainwater / stormwater strategy where appropriate.

## 8. Air & Thermal Standard

Human-use spaces:
- adequate fresh air;
- filtration appropriate to occupancy and local conditions;
- humidity management;
- quiet HVAC;
- no mining heat dumped into occupied areas unless engineered as useful heat recovery.

Mining / compute:
- independent heat path;
- negative or controlled pressure strategy where needed;
- dust control;
- intake filtration where appropriate;
- temperature monitoring;
- service shutoff.

## 10. Budget Design Allowances

Current planning allowances:
- Retrofit NEO House: $30,000 for NEO Estates Temple / Neotherapy environment.
- Purpose-Built NEO House: $60,000.
- Business / Warehouse: $50,000.

These allowances may cover:
- lighting;
- acoustic work;
- natural-material upgrades;
- Neotherapy room buildout;
- landscape;
- air-quality improvement;
- furnishings;
- controls.

They do not include major structural redesign unless specifically budgeted.

## 11. Development Review Gate

Before construction or acquisition approval, the project team should score:
- asset economics;
- life safety;
- code / zoning;
- accessibility;
- residential suitability;
- creator utility;
- Neotherapy / reflection quality;
- acoustic separation;
- mining / compute separation;
- power economics;
- landscape potential;
- operations;
- budget contingency.

## 12. Canonical Design Principle

A NEO Estate should feel:
**productive without feeling industrial; sacred without becoming theatrical; luxurious without waste; technologically advanced without sterility; restorative without making unsupported medical claims.**
