## Status Logic

### Green
- site-fit score 70+
- no unresolved fatal red flag
- underlying real-estate/business economics work without mining
- credible financing or partner path

### Amber
- score 55–69, or
- material diligence item unresolved, or
- financing gap, or
- zoning / utility / capex question pending

### Red
- score below 55, or
- fatal title / access / zoning / safety / contamination issue, or
- economics depend on Bitcoin appreciation or mining revenue to rescue the asset

## Mining Status

## Executive Review Cadence
Review live deals by:
- new scouts;
- deals awaiting score;
- deals above 70;
- deals with deadlines in the next 14 days;
- negotiation / contract deals;
- stalled deals;
- rejected deals with reusable lessons.

## Canonical Rule
No opportunity advances because it sounds exciting. It advances because the evidence clears the next gate.
