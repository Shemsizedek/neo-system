## Purpose
The Holy Stewards Build-to-Own Program creates a disciplined pathway by which qualified adult Temple adepts, apprentices, creators, builders and stewards can progress from training and service into practical housing competence, property stewardship and eventual ownership.

The program is intended to reinforce industrial skill, craftsmanship, enterprise, household responsibility and productive service to the wider community.

## Temple Doctrine Position
Grand Sheik Shemsizedek establishes Build-to-Own as a Science Temple™ incentive and development program for qualified A.C. Chamber participants.

The Temple interprets the building arts as a discipline of stewardship, maturity, productive citizenship and material self-reliance.

Jeremiah 4 does not literally command every man to build a house. The chapter speaks of repentance and uses agricultural imagery such as “break up your fallow ground.” Within this program, Branch Temple No. 24 may use that passage symbolically as a doctrine of preparing neglected ground, ordering one’s life, cultivating productive land and establishing useful works. This interpretation should be identified as Temple doctrine rather than presented as the plain historical meaning of the biblical chapter.

## Historical Foundation
The program draws from documented traditions of manual, industrial and vocational education rather than claiming an unsupported single Masonic homeownership rite.

Historically documented examples include:
- early-20th-century manuals teaching boys carpentry, design and architectural thinking;
- 1920 industrial-arts curricula for schools and colleges;
- trade-school programs teaching carpentry, electrical work, drafting and construction;
- 1923 small-house construction manuals written for people planning to build American homes;
- 1920s Masonic education encouraging apprentices toward vocational reading and study;
- operative-craft traditions in which apprentices learned the art and science of building from experienced craftsmen.

These sources support a broad historical connection among apprenticeship, craftsmanship, industrial education and building competence. They do not establish that all Masonic jurisdictions taught homeownership as a universal rite of manhood.

## Program Objective
Each qualified participant should progress toward the ability to:
1. understand land, housing and household economics;
2. read basic plans and scopes of work;
3. perform or supervise lawful building and maintenance work;
4. understand trades, permitting, safety and contracting;
5. participate in constructing or rehabilitating a NEO House;
6. maintain a property responsibly;
7. understand financing, insurance, taxes and reserves;
8. build an ownership plan;
9. provide quality products or services to the global market;
10. mentor the next steward.

## Build-to-Own Pathway

### Degree I — Foundation Steward
Focus:
- tool literacy;
- safety;
- basic carpentry;
- household maintenance;
- budgeting;
- housing history;
- NEO Estates standards;
- first-degree doctrine of building, stewardship and self-command.

Required product:
- complete a supervised repair / construction project;
- create a personal housing plan.

### Degree II — Industrial Steward
Focus:
- framing / carpentry fundamentals;
- electrical and plumbing awareness;
- materials;
- estimating;
- drawing / measurement;
- energy systems;
- site preparation;
- business and service quality.

Required product:
- participate in a NEO House buildout or rehabilitation;
- document hours and demonstrated competencies.

### Degree III — Property Steward
Focus:
- operations;
- maintenance;
- tenant / resident systems where applicable;
- Neotherapy Temple environment standards;
- creator / education spaces;
- budgeting and reserves;
- procurement;
- vendor management.

Required product:
- steward a property or defined property system under supervision.

### Degree IV — Build Partner
Focus:
- acquisition screening;
- site-fit scoring;
- construction management;
- financing;
- seller finance;
- project economics;
- legal / insurance / title awareness;
- partnerships.

Required product:
- participate in an acquisition, rehabilitation or ground-up development plan.

### Degree V — Build-to-Own Candidate
Focus:
- verified income / enterprise plan;
- savings / reserve plan;
- credit / financing readiness where relevant;
- land or property selection;
- construction or acquisition budget;
- sustainable carrying-cost analysis.

Outcome:
- candidate may pursue an ownership transaction under separate legal, financing and purchase documents.

Temple rank does not itself create title, equity, financing approval or a guaranteed property transfer.

## Ownership Models
Possible structures, subject to project-specific legal and tax review:
- direct purchase;
- seller financing;
- lease-to-own;
- shared-equity arrangement;
- cooperative or community ownership;
- sweat-equity credit documented under a lawful agreement;
- employer / developer assistance;
- land + modular / tiny / dome / yurt / earth-home build;
- rehabilitation-to-ownership;
- joint venture followed by buyout.

No ownership right exists until documented in enforceable project-specific agreements.

## Employment & Apprenticeship
The Moorish Science Temple / affiliated entities may employ, contract with or partner with qualified adepts for lawful work according to the actual role, compensation structure and applicable employment / contractor law.

Temple initiation, religious participation and housing ownership should be administratively separated from:
- wage entitlement;
- employment classification;
- contractor classification;
- sexual or intimate expectations;
- investment rights;
- title to property.

## Sweat Equity
Sweat equity may be recognized only when:
- scope is written;
- work is lawful;
- licensed trades are used where required;
- valuation method is documented;
- hours / deliverables are verified;
- tax and wage implications are reviewed;
- credits toward ownership are stated in a signed agreement.

Volunteer religious service should not be casually converted into hidden unpaid construction labor.
