# Shelton Estate & Co. — NEO Estates Design Standard

Status: Canonical planning standard
Module: NEO-HOUSES-001
Purpose: Neotherapy-centered architectural and environmental design layer for NEO Houses and related Shelton Estate & Co. developments.

## Positioning
NEO Estates applies Neotherapy Temple Healing aesthetics to houses, creator residences, student environments, commercial properties, hospitality, business acquisitions and warehouse conversions where appropriate.

The objective is not to represent buildings as medical clinics. The objective is to create built environments that support reflection, calm, creativity, fellowship, learning, wellness programming and dignified daily life.

## Core Design Formula
Clean air + controlled sound + intentional light + natural texture + contemplative space + productive technology + dignified beauty.

## Design Pillars

### Temple Light
- warm indirect lighting;
- daylight control;
- low-glare work and study lighting;
- programmable evening and circadian scenes;
- candle-like ambience where safe and appropriate.

### Sound & Quiet
- acoustic isolation;
- quiet rooms;
- meditation and reflection zones;
- separation of production, mechanical and mining noise from residential and wellness zones;
- optional sound-based Neotherapy environments presented as wellness experiences, not medical treatment.

### Living Materials
- stone;
- wood;
- copper accents;
- natural textiles;
- plants;
- water features;
- durable low-toxicity finishes where practical.

### Sacred Geometry
- symmetry and proportion;
- circular or radial gathering areas;
- intentional sightlines;
- geometric patterning inspired by Temple and NEO visual language.

### Healing Landscape
- courtyards;
- herbs and edible gardens;
- shade trees;
- walking paths;
- water elements;
- outdoor contemplation and teaching spaces.

### Neotherapy Rooms
Flexible rooms may support:
- guided reflection;
- relaxation;
- breathwork;
- Noological education;
- Neotherapy instruction;
- Reiki-inspired or energy-work practices;
- private wellness programming;
- small-group discussion.

These rooms are not represented as substitutes for licensed medical or mental-health treatment.

### Air & Thermal Quality
- ventilation;
- filtration;
- humidity control;
- thermal comfort;
- separation of heat-producing compute infrastructure from human-use zones.

### Technology Separation
Bitcoin miners and other heavy compute must remain electrically, thermally and acoustically segregated from:
- bedrooms;
- study rooms;
- healing/wellness rooms;
- classrooms;
- creator studios;
- gathering spaces.

Heat recovery may be evaluated where practical, but mining infrastructure must never degrade air quality, comfort, acoustics or safety.

## NEO Estates Asset Application

### Residential / Retrofit NEO House
Add a dedicated Temple environment allowance for lighting, acoustic treatment, natural materials, wellness room buildout, landscape and air-quality improvements.

### Purpose-Built NEO House
Integrate NEO Estates principles during site planning, architecture, landscape design and building systems rather than treating them as decorative upgrades after construction.

### Business / Warehouse
Create clearly separated human-centered zones for staff, creators, students, education, wellness and hospitality while compute, mechanical, loading and industrial operations remain physically segregated.

## Budget Allowances — Planning Baseline
- Acquisition + Retrofit NEO House: $30,000 NEO Estates Neotherapy / Temple environment allowance.
- Purpose-Built NEO House: $60,000 dedicated allowance.
- Business / Warehouse Acquisition: $50,000 wellness / Temple-environment buildout allowance.

These are planning allowances, not fixed construction bids.
