# NEO Houses - Shelton Estate & Co. Investment Package

Status: Canonical planning framework
Version: 1.0
Public page: https://holytemples.org/neo-houses/

## Thesis
NEO Houses combine residential real estate, creator development, education, entrepreneurship, service and disciplined productive-compute infrastructure. Shelton Estate & Co. applies the same model to houses, businesses and warehouses: acquire a fundamentally sound asset, improve it, then activate Bitcoin mining or other productive compute only when site-specific energy economics justify it.

## Operating Cycle
Learn -> Create -> Publish -> Collaborate -> Build -> Serve -> Lead

## Universal Acquisition Sequence
1. Scout.
2. Underwrite the core property/business.
3. Screen power, electrical capacity, demand charges, cooling, noise, fire and zoning.
4. Acquire and improve.
5. Activate approved residential, creator, educational or business use.
6. Activate mining only when economics clear.
7. Measure, document and replicate.

## Mining Underwriting Gate
Reference hardware: Bitmain S21 Pro, approximately 234 TH/s at 3.51 kW.
September 21, 2026 Hashrate Index snapshot: $40.93/PH/s/day.
Texas July 2026 EIA average electricity: residential 15.88 cents/kWh; commercial 8.47 cents/kWh; industrial 7.07 cents/kWh.

Planning thresholds:
- <= 6 cents/kWh: evaluate for continuous operation.
- 6-8 cents/kWh: conditional; current machine efficiency, hashprice, demand charges and cooling must clear.
- > 8 cents/kWh: normally remain mining-ready rather than mining-active.

No mining yield or Bitcoin price is guaranteed.

## Shelton Estate & Co. Fee Schedule
- Scouting and initial underwriting: $5,000 minimum.
- Acquisition execution/success: 1.5% of purchase price.
- Development/renovation management: 8% of managed capex.
- Mining/compute integration: 10% of mining-infrastructure capex; $5,000 minimum.
- Asset management: 1.25% annually of invested capital under management.
- Direct property/program operations: 8% of collected operating revenue.
- Creator/education administration: 10% of designated program revenue.
- NEO Village support allocation: target 2% of annual net operating surplus, subject to governing project documents.
- Capital placement/investor transaction fee: not offered until securities counsel confirms structure and required licensing/exemption.

## Participant Lanes
Creators: residence, production, portfolio, assignments and licensing.
Students/apprentices: living-learning, Noology, research, mentorship, entrepreneurship and service.
Investors/partners: asset-specific rights only under formal agreements and diligence.
NEO Villagers: support network through patronage, tenancy, referrals, service, commerce and qualified investment.

Temple or Society membership alone does not create an investment, employment, housing or economic right.

## Controls
- Never buy a weak asset because mining is expected to rescue it.
- No miner fleet without site electrical, utility, fire, ventilation, acoustics and insurance review.
- No guaranteed mining, Bitcoin or investment return claims.
- Housing, apprenticeship, creator opportunity and Temple participation stay separate from investment rights.
- Pooled capital, investor interests, profit shares and token interests require project-specific legal review.
- Every asset requires its own underwriting file, reserve policy and exit assumptions.
